=== CHAPTER 008 VOICEOVER SCRIPT ===
Total Shots: 54 | Words: 1213

--- [RECORDING VERSION: CONTINUOUS PROSE] ---
High on the royal balcony, Duke Aden lays down the law: this is a formal evaluation spar, meaning blunted wooden weapons only. And more importantly, Aden explicitly forbids Babel from using mana—because pitting a mana-empowered C-rank knight against an unawakened kid is basic murder. Babel scoffs under his breath, thinking that wasting a single drop of mana on a manure-shoveling bastard is beneath his noble dignity anyway. But our boy Joshua reads the room instantly: Babel and Chiffon orchestrated this entire circus to humiliate him in front of continental royalty. From the balcony, Princess Charles laughs out loud, thoroughly amused that the concubine's son brought a literal walking stick to a sword duel! Count Chiffon mocks the boy openly, asking what on earth he expects to accomplish with a peasant's stick against an imperial heir's blade. Duke Aden locks eyes with his youngest son, asking with grave intensity: 'Are you being serious?' Our boy answers with a single word: 'Yes.' The Duke takes his seat and roars the command: 'NOW, LET THE SPAR BEGIN!' Babel doesn't hold back for a fraction of a second. He launches forward like a cannonball, swinging his practice sword in a vicious downward diagonal slash! To any normal spectator, Joshua looks frozen in terror. But our boy waits until the wood is inches from his collarbone before making his move. Clack, clack, clack! With three microscopic wrist rotations, Joshua redirects Babel's entire momentum into empty floor space! Babel stumbles off-balance, snarling that the stable rat must have learned some cheap parlor tricks from traveling peddlers. But Joshua is already dissecting him: *He has decent C-rank physical speed, but his tactical mind is as predictable as a spoiled toddler.* Sliding his hands along the wood, Joshua extends his reach by four feet, slipping past Babel's clumsy broadsword guard with surgical precision. Whack! He drives the blunt wooden tip straight into Babel's solar plexus, knocking the wind clean out of the older brother's lungs with a sickening 'OOFT!' Babel staggers backward five paces, coughing violently. The entire spectator gallery freezes in pure shock—the fourteen-year-old heir just got countered! Humiliated in front of Princess Charles, Lady Isellin, and his legendary father, Babel's fragile teenage sanity completely snaps. Screaming that a concubine's bastard has no right to challenge him, Babel breaks the Duke's rules and ignites raw mana along his wooden sword! 'You should rot in the stable where you belong!' Babel roars, lunging to decapitate his little brother! Duke Aden roars from the balcony: 'BABEL! STOP!' But Joshua doesn't flinch. At point-blank range, retreating is suicide. If Babel wants to play with mana, our boy will show him what real magic looks like. With a terrifying intake of breath, Joshua unleashes the ancient technique! Pure sapphire mana explodes around his wooden spear like a roaring tempest! Count Ponselle—the 5th-Class imperial archmage—leaps out of his seat in total shock, shouting that the supposed cripple is wielding high-density mana! Babel's arrogant expression instantly dissolves into horror. His tiny flame is swallowed whole by Joshua's roaring azure hurricane! Joshua executes Magic Spear Arts Stage 1! His wooden spear shears clean through Babel's sword, pulverizing the seasoned oak into flying splinters! The shockwave hurls Babel twenty feet across the arena, smashing his spine against the stone wall with a concussive boom: 'GUAAAHHH!' Our boy steps forward to finish the job—until a golden tidal wave of pure Master-level pressure crashes down from above! Duke Aden drops from the balcony like a meteor, releasing his colossal master aura and commanding them to freeze: 'STOP! I will not forgive another move!' The sonic pressure rattles the arena chandeliers. Princess Charles covers her ears in shock, while Lady Isellin stares at Joshua in complete awe. Up on the terrace, Count Chiffon is trembling so hard his teeth chatter: 'Mana at age nine?! This is unheard of in continental history!' Count Ponselle steps down, verifying that Joshua's mana density is vastly superior to Babel's! This isn't just an awakening—it's a generational anomaly! Sir Cain stands in the shadows with an ecstatic grin. His instincts were completely vindicated—Joshua is the true dragon of the Agnus bloodline! Duke Aden strides across the splintered floorboards, staring down at his nine-year-old son with an expression he has never shown another living soul. Chiffon desperately tries to bring up the curse again, but Duke Aden cuts him down with a single murderous look: 'Chiffon, watch your mouth.' Then Duke Aden drops the command that breaks the entire estate hierarchy: 'Joshua... come to my private study tonight. I have something to ask you.' Babel clutches his broken ribs in agony, his mind shattered: Duke Aden has never once invited anyone—not even his legitimate heir—to his private sanctum! Our boy lowers his practice spear with flawless military grace, bowing slightly: 'I understand. I will see you tonight, Duke Agnus.' Fast-forward to the Pontier family's private suite. Princess Charles is literally jumping up and down on the velvet mattress like a kid who just won the lottery! 'Cox! That was absolutely incredible!' she squeals. 'A nine-year-old commoner's bastard smashing the empire's greatest heir with a wooden stick?!' Her guardian Cox begs her to calm down, but Charles crosses her arms and announces: 'Alright, I've made my decision!' 'Forget Babel,' the merchant princess declares with hungry ambition. 'Joshua is the true diamond of this empire. I am going to make that boy mine!' Meanwhile, across the garden in the Rebreka guest wing, Lady Isellin sits silently by the window with an ancient grimoire open in her lap. She stares at the diagrams of the fatal Mana Curse, unable to reconcile the textbooks with the godlike sapphire tempest she witnessed with her own eyes. Her father, Count Ponselle, paces the room in agitation, realizing that Joshua's mana frequency wasn't imperial knight aura—it was ancient primordial energy! As twilight gives way to midnight, the ducal estate falls into an uneasy silence heavy with brewing conflict. Our boy Joshua walks down the executive corridor toward Duke Aden's private sanctum, carrying the dormant spirit of Lugia under his coat. He knows the Duke wants answers. But Joshua doesn't plan on giving away his secrets for free—he plans on using this meeting to secure ducal resources. Knock, knock. Joshua taps on the massive ironwood doors, and the gravelly voice of the empire's greatest warlord calls out: 'Enter.' The door swings open into a dimly lit study filled with war maps, ancient trophies, and the legendary two-meter greatsword resting against the mantle. Duke Aden swirls the amber liquid in his glass, gesturing toward the leather armchair opposite him: 'Take a seat, Joshua.' Our boy sits down with the relaxed composure of an equal monarch, meeting his father's icy blue gaze with complete stillness. The Duke doesn't waste time with small talk: 'Where on earth did an untrained nine-year-old learn master-tier spear arts that shattered an imperial knight?' Joshua smiles faintly into the crackling flames, preparing a reply that will secure his independence and shake the empire's foundation. While in the shadows of the east wing, Duchess Vanessa and Count Chiffon are already drafting a deadly retaliation to eliminate the awakened bastard. An illegitimate son playing chess against the greatest warlord alive, two princesses scheming to claim him, and the true imperial war about to begin.
