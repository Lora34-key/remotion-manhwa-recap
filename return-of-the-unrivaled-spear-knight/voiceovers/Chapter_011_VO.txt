=== CHAPTER 011 VOICEOVER SCRIPT ===
Total Shots: 54 | Words: 1258

--- [RECORDING VERSION: CONTINUOUS PROSE] ---
Over at Duke Agnus's central military training hall, dozens of elite centurions are huddled together, trading scandalous rumors in hushed whispers. Two veteran squad leaders corner Rols against the stone wall, demanding to know where his bully partners Gort and Roid suddenly disappeared to. Ever since watching his friends get executed like cattle, Rols has been completely mute, sweating bullets every time someone mentions Joshua's name. Suddenly, the massive oak doors swing wide open as a herald's voice booms across the arena: Red Knight Sir Cain has entered the building! Every soldier immediately snaps to attention, expecting a grueling inspection—only to blink in confusion at the small kid casually strolling beside him. Whispers break out across the ranks—that's the barn bastard Joshua! While the others scoff, Rols looks like he's about to faint on the spot. Sir Cain doesn't hesitate. With full ducal authority, he announces that today's entire combat evaluation will be run by none other than Joshua von Agnus! Our boy Joshua steps into the center of the hall, declaring with absolute authority that he is here to evaluate their combat readiness. The veteran centurions are completely insulted. They are battle-hardened squad leaders who command the vanguard, not babysitters for a toddler. Joshua cuts right through their murmurs, reminding them that leading troops into war requires sheer individual strength, not inflated egos. Then our boy drops the most disrespectful challenge imaginable: he tells the entire squad of centurions to draw their swords and jump him together! The centurions scoff, warning him that royal blood or not, swinging real blades against hardened soldiers will only end with him in the medical ward. Joshua doesn't even blink. He casually picks up a long training spear from the rack, spinning the heavy shaft like a feather between his fingers. He marches straight over and points the steel spearhead dead center at Rols's neck, asking if he also thinks this is a childish game. Rols collapses to his knees like a sack of potatoes, frantically begging for mercy in front of all his bewildered peers! Joshua's expression turns razor-sharp. He gave Rols one simple task—warn the barracks what happens to anyone who touches his mother—and Rols choked. The spearhead cracks the stone right between Rols's boots! Joshua asks him out loud if he wants to share the exact same fate as Gort and Roid. Hearing Gort and Roid's names makes the entire room go dead silent. The officers realize with mounting horror that the rumors were real. Joshua orders Rols to his feet and tells him to draw steel. If he can defeat a nine-year-old in single combat, his life will be spared. Cornered with no way out, Rols's survival instincts kick in. He reasons that no matter how prodigal Joshua is, a child's muscles cannot stop a grown warrior's full charge! Rols draws his broadsword with a frantic roar, throwing his entire body weight into a desperate, overhead execution slash! Our boy doesn't even bother dodging. Azure mana surges along the wooden spear shaft, crackling like living lightning around the tip! With a flick of his wrist, he unleashes the Magic Spear Arts First Form: Lightning Strike! The blunt butt of the spear catches Rols square in the chest before his blade even reaches the halfway mark, blowing clean through his reinforced cuirass! Rols is catapulted thirty meters through the air, embedding into the far stone wall and slumping unconscious without even landing a single blow! The centurions stand frozen like statues. That wasn't just physical strength—that was refined, condensed mana channeled through an ordinary spear! Joshua casually rests the spear over his shoulder, lecturing the room: judging an adversary by their size or age is the fastest way to end up in a body bag. He dares anyone who still doubts his authority to step into the ring. Not a single man moves an inch. The senior-most officer steps forward, drops his sword to the stone, and falls to one knee, loudly swearing fealty to Young Master Joshua! A glorious domino effect ripples through the hall! One by one, every squad commander drops to their knees, bowing their heads in complete surrender. By the doors, Sir Cain watches with starry eyes. In less than ten minutes, a nine-year-old has thoroughly subjugated the fiercest division in the ducal army. Meanwhile, over in the guest palace, Princess Cheryl is curled up in bed clutching her stuffed puppy, still replaying Joshua's brutal rejection in her head. 'Screw off... Joshua,' she mumbles into her pillow. She's supposed to be an untouchable high noble heiress, but that little demon boy made her feel completely helpless! Her guardian Cox bursts through the door with an urgent update: the imperial court has officially dispatched the Royal Knight Order to the Agnus estate! Cheryl groans, telling him to get lost. Cox reminds her that Duke Pontier sent them on a diplomatic mission, and slacking off will ruin everything. But political treaties are the last thing on her mind. Staring at the ceiling, she whispers that all she wants is to figure out who Joshua really is. The scene cuts to Duke Aden's study. The Master of the Nine Stars looks up from his ledgers as Joshua steps inside to report. Aden mentions that reports already reached his desk about Joshua violently demolishing a squad leader in the central hall. Joshua doesn't flinch. He explains that under imperial martial law, defaming nobility carries the death penalty—and Aden himself would have severed his head. Aden admits that's true, but wonders if Joshua acted out of reckless childish temper or calculated strategy. Our boy lays out the brutal truth: just a few days ago, he was a nameless barn bastard. Sudden respect cannot be bought with sweet smiles. The only way to guarantee his mother's safety while he's away at the capital is to plant absolute, overwhelming terror into every soldier's mind. Duke Aden lets out a satisfied chuckle. Joshua has the ruthless mind of an apex predator. He tells him to get ready—the imperial testing party has arrived! Down by the grand courtyard gates, Count Chiffon is pacing anxiously, grumbling about how the Agnus estate suddenly produced two rival C-rank prodigies. Other commanders mock Chiffon, asking if he's sweating because his favorite horse Babel was just put out to pasture by a nine-year-old child. The bickering instantly ceases as Duke Aden arrives at the courtyard with Joshua at his side, stepping out onto the grand promenade. The camera pulls back to reveal an astonishing spectacle: an enormous royal envoy stretches across the bridge, flanked by hundreds of elite imperial cavalry! Chiffon is flabbergasted—did the capital dispatch an entire knight battalion just to administer a routine rank test to a child?! The gilded door swings open with a heavy thud. A polished leather boot steps onto the stone pavement, commanding the presence of high royalty. Stepping into the sunlight is a handsome, golden-haired royal dressed in pristine military regalia, gazing across the estate with patronizing amusement. Duke Aden's jaw tightens. He instantly recognizes that face—it is none other than the Fourth Prince of the Avalon Empire! Kaiser von Britton—the treacherous schemer who orchestrated the second civil war, stole the throne, and had Joshua murdered in his previous life! The moment Joshua sees that deceitful face, centuries of bottled-up hatred explode inside his chest like a volcanic eruption! His sworn enemy has arrived on his doorstep years ahead of schedule. The architect of his death is standing right in front of him—and Joshua vows to make him taste true despair!
