=== CHAPTER 012 VOICEOVER SCRIPT ===
Total Shots: 54 | Words: 1210

--- [RECORDING VERSION: CONTINUOUS PROSE] ---
Before the imperial delegation even arrived at the ducal estate, dark machinations were already turning behind closed doors in the capital. Inside the Imperial Knight barracks, a junior recruit is violently beaten half to death, begging for mercy at the boots of a sadistic officer. Meet Joker, Captain of the 1st Imperial Battalion—a notoriously ruthless B-rank veteran who despises rookies and loves breaking bones for fun. A hooded agent emerges from the darkness with a discreet royal bounty directly from Duchess Vanessa: a classified hit job on the Agnus estate. Joker grins from ear to ear. His orders are simple: disguise the evaluation as an accident and permanently shatter Joshua's mana core and limbs! The camera cuts back to the present courtyard, where Prince Kaiser steps onto the promenade, locking eyes with our boy Joshua. Standing only five paces away, Joshua stares at the man who manipulated his loyalty, betrayed his army, and ordered his death in his previous life. An imperial retainer immediately scolds Joshua, barking that a low-born child must drop to both knees in the presence of royal blood. Joshua laughs right in his face. He points out imperial law: there cannot be two suns in the empire, and a knight answers only to the Emperor! He drops the ultimate insult, asking why anyone should bow to a powerless Fourth Prince whose claim to the throne isn't even guaranteed! Imperial Vice-Captain Valmont draws his sword in absolute fury, roaring that a kid who was shoveling horse manure yesterday has completely lost his mind! Even Count Chiffon steps forward with drawn steel, declaring that regardless of birth, an Agnus cannot be assaulted on their own front lawn! Suddenly, the entire courtyard shakes! A suffocating mountain of azure mana erupts as Duke Aden steps forward like an ancient god of war! Aden's voice chills everyone's marrow: 'Drawing blades before royalty... do you people wish to die right now?' Valmont freezes in absolute terror. Aden tells Joshua that disrespecting imperial guests is unacceptable and demands an apology to smooth over political protocol. But Prince Kaiser waves his hand with a saintly smile. He tells the Duke not to worry, admitting that as the fourth son, he holds no actual sovereignty. Kaiser walks right up to Joshua, gently resting a hand on his shoulder while delivering a veiled barb: a true knight speaks through steel, not empty words. Joshua recognizes that venomous, hypocritical smile. He vows internally that he won't assassinate Kaiser today—he will decapitate him at the height of his power! Our boy simply responds that he is always ready to dance, casually strolling toward the evaluation grounds like he owns the place. Sensing his golden opportunity, Captain Joker steps forward, humbly volunteering to personally oversee Joshua's certification duel. Kaiser readily agrees, praising Joker's B-rank pedigree and granting him full authority to conduct the formal combat test. Turning toward the arena, Joker's grin widens into a demonic sneer. He promises himself that this kid will leave the ring on a stretcher. The arena is set. High above on the marble balcony sit Duke Aden, Kaiser, Chief Wizard Evergrant, and the imperial commanders. Down in the ring, Joshua enters wielding a blunt steel training pole. Joker immediately bursts out laughing at the pathetic excuse for a weapon. Joker barks that bringing a rod to a knight's duel is an insult to the imperial crown. Joshua yawns, noting that the imperial order sure loves to talk. Our boy wonders aloud if imperial knights are selected for combat prowess or their ability to recite speeches. Joker's jaw practically snaps from rage! Thick cyan mana erupts around Joker's blade! He charges with true B-rank speed, determined to carve the insolent brat into ribbons! The blades collide with a bone-jarring crash! Shockwaves rip through the flagstones as Joshua blocks the B-rank strike dead in its tracks! Joker's eyes bulge in shock. Joshua's mana isn't just defending—it feels denser and heavier than a collapsing mountain! Joker tries to exploit the classic spear weakness by closing the gap to point-blank range, expecting an easy kill inside Joshua's guard. But Joshua laughs in his face. In his past life, he mastered close-quarters spear combat centuries ago, neutralizing the dead zone completely! He channels the Magic Spear Arts First Form: Lightning Strike, swinging the iron pole like a crescent bolt of thunder! The blow smashes into Joker's pauldron, sending the grown captain stumbling backward in front of the entire imperial entourage! Up on the balcony, Valmont can't believe what he's seeing: a nine-year-old kid is trading blows on equal footing with an imperial battalion captain! Valmont realizes the horrifying truth: Joshua is intentionally holding his mana back to C-rank, yet he is utterly outclassing Joker in pure martial technique! Five rapid-fire exchanges detonate across the tiles in less than two seconds, every spear parry deflecting Joker's sword with surgical precision. Panic sets in for Joker. If this match ends as an honorable draw, Duchess Vanessa will execute him for failing to cripple the target. Pushed to the brink, Joker decides to discard all knightly honor and gamble his entire career on an illegal, lethal strike! Joshua feels the strain in his young joints. His tactical mind is that of a grandmaster, but his prepubescent body cannot endure prolonged high-intensity exertion. Joker roars like a mad beast, forcibly unleashing forbidden internal energy to spike his aura far beyond normal B-rank regulations! Up on the gallery, Count Chiffon leaps to his feet, shouting that Joker's power has crossed the line into an outright assassination attempt! But Duke Aden holds up a hand, commanding everyone to stay put. The master wants to see how his son handles true mortal peril. Joker dives downward with full execution force, screaming that Joshua's flimsy spear tricks won't save him from being crushed into paste! Joshua anchors his heels into the floorboards. Deep cobalt electricity crackles along his arms as he prepares to meet the killing stroke head-on! Joker brings the massive broadsword down with decapitating malice, convinced the annoying little prodigy is about to be erased from existence! Valmont roars at Joker to stand down, but the rogue captain has already committed everything to the kill! An earth-shattering detonation engulfs the center of the ring! A tidal wave of primordial energy violently blows back the entire arena! Even Duke Aden grips the balcony railing so hard his fingers crack the solid marble, his eyes widening in total disbelief! Valmont and Prince Kaiser are forced to shield their eyes as divine pressure washes over the entire hall like an ancient awakening! When the dust begins to clear, a massive fifty-meter trench has been gouged straight through the reinforced stone arena! Skidding at the far end of the trench is Captain Joker, clutching his trembling sword with blood dripping from his lips, unable to comprehend what just hit him! 'I was pushed back?!' Joker stammers in horror. 'What kind of demonic monstrosity is standing before me?!' Through the parting smoke stands our boy Joshua, completely untouched. The plain iron rod has shattered its disguise! Blazing in his grip is the sacred divine spear Lugia, crackling with crimson and white lightning! The unrivaled spear knight has revealed his divine fang—and the imperial court will never be the same!
