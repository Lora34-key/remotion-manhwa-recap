=== CHAPTER 013 VOICEOVER SCRIPT ===
Total Shots: 54 | Words: 1295

--- [RECORDING VERSION: CONTINUOUS PROSE] ---
Right in the middle of Joker's illegal execution strike, our boy Joshua channels the deepest frequencies of his mana core, summoning the sacred name: Lugia! The disguise on the iron rod violently shatters! A blinding flash of divine aura detonates outward, blasting the imperial captain fifty paces across the arena! Through the dissipating smoke stands a nine-year-old child brandishing a legendary weapon—the divine spear Lugia, gleaming in pristine white and crimson steel. The entire spectator gallery loses its collective mind. High nobles whisper in shock: has a nine-year-old somehow bonded with a genuine ancient artifact?! Prince Kaiser breaks into a polite smile, leaning over to Vice-Captain Valmont and noting that Joshua has already proven far more than required for a C-rank test. But down in the ring, Joker is consumed by panic. If he doesn't cripple the boy now, Duchess Vanessa will execute him, so he prepares to charge again! Before Joker can take another step, Valmont drops from the balcony like a red thunderbolt, drawing his sword and commanding Joker to stand down immediately! Valmont stares at our boy in sheer disbelief. Even Babel's legendary genius pales into utter insignificance compared to what Joshua just displayed. With full formal dignity, Valmont raises the imperial decree, projecting his voice across the arena to deliver the empire's official verdict! 'Joshua von Agnus,' Valmont roars, 'by imperial decree, I officially declare your appointment as a C-Rank Knight! Kneel before the blade of the Emperor!' Joshua doesn't grovel or kneel. He stands tall with Lugia resting over his shoulder, calmly accepting the title like a sovereign accepting a token tribute. Prince Kaiser leads the applause with an elegant smile. But behind those glittering amber eyes, he is calculating just how dangerous this boy might become. Joshua stares straight back at Kaiser. In this lifetime, he won't be anyone's sacrificial pawn; he will make every betrayer suffer tenfold! A warm fur cloak suddenly drops over Joshua's shoulders. He looks up to find Duke Aden standing right behind him, softly calling his name. 'Are you hurt?' Aden asks quietly. Joshua is momentarily stunned—in fifty years of his previous life, he had never once seen his father look at him with genuine concern. Shaking off the sentimentality, Joshua replies that he is perfectly fine, reaching out to grasp the ceremonial knight's sword presented by the empire. He hoists the blade high into the air, formally cementing history: at nine years old, Joshua von Agnus has become the youngest certified knight on the continent! But the celebration is short-lived. That very night, emergency torches line the corridor outside the ducal grand council chamber. Duke Aden sits at the head of the colossal mahogany council table, surrounded by his vassal lords and knights for a mandatory high-level assembly. Count Rannel rises to deliver the opening report: the Knights of the Golden Mane, sent on a northern border pacification campaign, have returned early. Aden questions why an elite expedition retreated prematurely. Rannel explains that monster activity along the northern frontier hasn't just increased—it has multiplied exponentially! Entire outposts are being wiped off the map overnight, with veteran squads torn to shreds before they can even send out distress flares! The nightmare culprit is the apex predator of the northern woods: the Black Ogre, a mutated titan whose skin is tougher than tempered steel! Even high-tier Fourth Circle battle mages and veteran B-rank knights struggle to bring down a single Black Ogre without heavy casualties. While the lords panic, Duke Aden shuts them down with one cold sentence: he will personally step into the field and eliminate the beasts himself. Rannel moves to the second agenda item regarding the Pontier family's visit, but Aden instructs him to hold that briefing for a private meeting later. Before the meeting can adjourn, Count Chiffon raises his hand, announcing that he has an urgent concern regarding Joshua's sudden appointment! Several lords remark that having the continent's youngest knight brings unprecedented glory to Agnus, but Chiffon quickly rains on their parade. Chiffon reminds everyone of the cold facts: Joshua lived in a barn with a known mana disability. How does a disabled child suddenly become a C-rank overnight?! He argues passionately that natural genius cannot defy biology, claiming there must be a sinister, external explanation behind this miracle. He points to the weapon: how could a nine-year-old wield a shapeshifting spear that radiates alien power unless he contracted with a demonic entity?! Dark magic is an absolute continent-wide taboo. If the imperial court suspects an Agnus son of dabbling in demonic power, the entire ducal bloodline could face holy crusade! Chiffon smugly suggests exiling Joshua to a remote northern outpost until an investigation is concluded, effectively burying him out of sight. SLAM! The council chamber doors burst wide open as every lord snaps their head around to see our boy Joshua standing on the threshold! With hands tucked into his pockets, Joshua strolls right down the center of the hall, repeating Chiffon's words with dripping venom: 'Demonic energy?' He marches right up to Chiffon's desk, daring the grown commander to draw his rapier and test whether his power is demonic with a duel right now! Chiffon is paralyzed. A nine-year-old child is openly threatening to cross blades with a ducal commander in the middle of a high council session! Chiffon breaks into a cold sweat. If Joshua truly fought on par with Joker, accepting this challenge could end with Chiffon getting butchered in front of his peers! Joshua doesn't let him breathe, roasting him in front of every lord: shouldn't one of the family's finest commanders have the spine to back up his slander?! Pushed past his limit, Chiffon violently kicks his chair back, unleashing his aura and screaming that Joshua has crossed every line of decency! The surrounding lords scramble out of their seats, shouting for Chiffon to stand down before he triggers a bloodbath in the council chambers! Duke Aden taps his index finger on the mahogany table. The single sound rings like a thunderclap, freezing both combatants dead in their tracks! Aden fixes Chiffon with a chilling stare, pointing out that in his frantic rush to slander Joshua, he overlooked one undeniable fact. Chief Magician Evergrant of the Imperial Order personally oversaw the duel. If Joshua had used even a microgram of demonic energy, Evergrant would have purged him instantly! Chiffon slumps back into his chair, completely defeated. With no counterargument left, Aden declares the slander closed for good. But Duke Aden isn't done. He turns his heavy gaze back onto Joshua, noting that clearing demonic charges doesn't explain his impossible rate of growth. Aden admits that seeing a nine-year-old child wield Master-tier spear mechanics is genuinely unprecedented, even for an apex warrior of the Nine Stars. To truly measure what his son is capable of when pushed to the absolute brink, Duke Aden drops a shocking proposal that silences the entire room. 'We will be heading to the Forest of Dark Beings,' Aden declares—taking Joshua straight into the terrifying hunting ground of the Black Ogres! The council lords gasp in horror. Throwing a nine-year-old into the deadliest monster-infested wilderness on the continent sounds like a suicide mission! But our boy Joshua doesn't flinch for a second. A slow, dangerous smile curls across his lips as he welcomes the trial with open arms. Facing apex monsters is exactly what he needs to temper his nine-year-old vessel and unlock the higher stages of the Magic Spear Arts! Father and son lock eyes across the council hall, both understanding that words are meaningless—only blood and steel will reveal the truth. The stage is set. An apex ducal expedition, an army of mutated Black Ogres, and a nine-year-old reaper ready to paint the forest red. The hunt begins now!
