=== CHAPTER 017 VOICEOVER SCRIPT ===
Total Shots: 54 | Words: 1185

--- [RECORDING VERSION: CONTINUOUS PROSE] ---
As twilight bathes the northern peaks in fiery amber light, Duke Aden orders his forces to halt and pitch camp on the open plain. Viscount Beeg waddles over in a panic, whining about why they are pitching tents in an exposed field instead of marching straight into the trees. Old knight Roben scoffs under his breath. The corrupt viscount knows everything about taxing peasants and zero about basic military strategy. Duke Aden shuts the coward down, educating him on the deadly reality of the northern woods when the sun dips below the horizon. Under the rising Red Moon, monster aggression spikes fivefold! Marching into dense foliage in the dark is an invitation to be slaughtered in an ambush. Realizing he just looked like an idiot, Beeg immediately switches to shameless flattery, praising the Duke's infinite strategic genius. Aden orders Babel and Joshua to pitch their own tents, treating them not as coddled high nobles, but as disciplined soldiers in the field. Under the clever excuse of gathering dry firewood for the camp, our boy Joshua steps into the dense tree line accompanied by a local guard. Joshua casually dismisses the guard, telling him to take the first bundle back to camp while he gathers a few more thick logs. The second the soldier disappears behind the brush, Joshua drops the twigs, turning and sprinting like a shadow into the forbidden heart of the forest! But Joshua wasn't entirely unobserved. Lurking behind an oak tree is Viscount Beeg, watching the young boy vanish into the darkness with a sinister sneer! Why is our boy risking his neck alone? Lore reveals the answer: he is hunting for an ancient relic known as the Primordial Stone! Born of pure primordial mana, these five legendary stones can instantly elevate a novice magician's spells by two full circles without strain! But for Joshua, its value is life-saving: only a Primordial Stone can stabilize his overflowing adult-tier mana and prevent his young core from bursting! In his previous life, explorers discovered one of the stones buried in the heart of this exact forest years later. Joshua is here to claim it first! After an hour of traversing treacherous terrain, the canopy thickens into near-total darkness, blotting out the stars completely. Heavy rhythmic tremors vibrate through the earth—THUD... THUD... Joshua freezes, instantly dropping flat behind a mossy log. Lumbering directly onto the central trail is a massive mutated Black Ogre, blocking the only direct route to the forest's sacred heart! Black Ogres shrug off low-grade magic like rain and require B-rank knights to bring down. Fighting one head-on right now will blow his cover and waste precious stamina. Joshua agilely scales a towering pine, deciding to deploy the secret stealth art of his past-life companion: the legendary Assassin King! Unlike combat arts that concentrate mana into sharp focal points, assassin stealth diffuses mana into a thin, imperceptible membrane around the skin. Joshua slows his heartbeat to one beat per minute, extinguishing his breath and matching his body temperature perfectly to the winter breeze. The Black Ogre stops inches beneath his perch, sniffing the air with confused, snorting grunts—smelling nothing but pine needles and damp earth! Finding no scent of prey, the beast lumbers away into the ravine. Joshua exhales softly—the Assassin King's technique still works like a charm! Joshua leaps across the high branches like an ethereal cat, bypassing the monster-infested forest floor entirely! But continuously maintaining full-body mana diffusion while performing high-speed gymnastics places enormous stress on a nine-year-old cardiovascular system. Disaster strikes! Landing on an ancient rotten bough, the wood fractures under his weight with a loud, echoing CRACK! Joshua plummets twelve meters through the canopy, crashing onto the hard frozen ground and bruising his ribs and spine! The loud crash instantly shatters the forest's silence. Hungry rustling sounds erupt from every bush surrounding the clearing! Over twenty berserk goblins and feral orcs step into the moonlight, brandishing rusted cleavers and baring blood-soaked fangs! Encircled with his back against a boulder, our boy sighs, muttering that an unconditioned body really makes basic mobility annoying. The first goblin lunges with saliva spraying from its jaws, thrusting a jagged poisoned dagger straight toward Joshua's neck! But instead of panicking, Joshua's eyes light up with pure exhilaration. He has been dying for a target dummy to test out his upgraded spear forms! He drops into a rock-solid horse stance, gripping the spear shaft horizontally with both hands as azure mana begins to vortex around his waist! Gale-force winds violently whip up around his boots! The air pressure drops drastically as compressed mana spirals into a howling vortex! Joshua whispers the ancient invocation: 'Magic Spear Arts, Second Phase, Second Form: Violent Gale!' He spins the spear in a full three-hundred-and-sixty-degree arc! A colossal cyclone of razor-sharp mana blades detonates thirty paces in every direction! The hurricane of energy slices through reinforced armor, iron bucklers, and monster bones like hot wire through butter, detonating the entire pack into red mist! The shockwave is so violent that twenty massive pine trees are cleanly sheared in half, crashing to the forest floor around the clearing! When the storm settles, not a single enemy remains breathing. The entire twenty-monster ambush was wiped from existence in a single heartbeat! Joshua checks his core: the Second Phase consumed barely twenty percent of his reserves. His temporal knot is loosening, unlocking greater efficiency! But as the ringing in his ears fades, Joshua picks up the faint sound of panicked human breathing hiding behind the shattered tree trunks. Crouched behind a boulder is Viscount Beeg, his round face white as a sheet, trembling violently after witnessing a nine-year-old butcher an army in seconds! The fat noble is practically wetting his trousers. If that demon boy realizes he was stalking him, Joshua could erase him right here and blame it on the monsters! Joshua deliberately plays along, pretending he didn't notice the fat lord trembling behind the rock. Let the corrupt coward stew in his own terror! Ahead in the mist, a soft, ethereal luminescence pulses from a hidden cavern—the undeniable aura of the Primordial Stone! Stepping inside the subterranean grotto, ancient crystalline formations glow along the cavern ceiling like a subterranean galaxy. Hovering above an underground spring is the legendary Primordial Stone of Storms, crackling with pure primal lightning! Joshua reaches out his hand. The moment Lugia's frequency harmonizes with the stone, the subterranean chamber illuminates in glorious sapphire light! The stone dissolves into liquid lightning, pouring straight through Joshua's palm into his spiritual core to permanently stabilize his power! A golden chime echoes in his soul as the temporal blockage completely unravels! His meridians expand, permanently cementing his core stability! Dawn approaches as military horns echo from the camp perimeter, signaling that Duke Aden's vanguard is preparing to enter the forest. Joshua steps out of the cavern with crystal-clear vision. His vessel has ascended, and his spear is ready to challenge even the Nine Stars! An unraveled seal, a petrified corrupt lord, and an unrivaled spear master about to show Duke Aden what true genius looks like. The campaign marches on!
