=== CHAPTER 019 VOICEOVER SCRIPT ===
Total Shots: 54 | Words: 1209

--- [RECORDING VERSION: CONTINUOUS PROSE] ---
Right after obliterating the ambushing monster pack, our boy Joshua treks deeper into a jagged subterranean gorge, following a faint electromagnetic hum. Standing at the cliff edge, his hair flutters in the charged static air, guided by the memories of his previous life's northern campaigns. Deep down in the fissure, a colossal crystalline spire pulses with untamed celestial lightning—the legendary Primordial Stone of Storms, Bronto! This is the divine anchor he desperately needed. His nine-year-old mana circuits were on the verge of collapsing from explosive technique backlash. Breathing in rhythm with Lugia's hidden hum, Joshua siphons a refined strand of Bronto's frequency directly into his core, harmonizing his unstable energy. But miles away at the expedition encampment, the blood moon burns with sinister crimson light, amplifying the madness of every northern beast. Even after Duke Aden's catastrophic sword strike cleaved a hundred-meter trench, the atmosphere in the forward camp remains suffocatingly tense. Meanwhile, Babel is still shivering from his near-death encounter, but Count Rebrecca immediately swoops in with sickening noble flattery. The fat count laughs that only the legitimate heir could ever carry Duke Aden's invincible legacy, inflating Babel's battered ego back to maximum levels. But glancing around the perimeter, Rebrecca realizes someone critical is missing from the muster ranks—our boy Joshua is nowhere to be seen! Marching over to Chiffon, Rebrecca sneers loudly, asking where that insolent concubine's brat disappeared to during the chaotic assault. Nursing his cracked ribs, Chiffon admits he lost track of the boy, instantly sparking malicious rumors across the tired guard battalions. Rebrecca openly laughs, declaring that despite all Joshua's arrogant posturing, when real monsters showed up, the commoner's brat deserted to save his own skin! The camp soldiers immediately jump on the gossip bandwagon, claiming Joshua's lowborn upbringing finally proved he has zero warrior backbone. But before their slanders can spread another inch, Duke Aden turns with an icy glare that freezes the blood of every gossiping noble in their tracks! Aden roars at his men to drop the petty court politics—a seasoned commander knows when the forest's blood lust is reaching its boiling point! Right on cue, the earth beneath their boots begins to tremble violently, tossing iron kettles and weapons across the frozen mud! Massive pine trunks splinter and snap along the ridge, as dozens of crimson eyes ignite through the swirling nocturnal mist! An entire vanguard of mutated Black Ogres bursts through the outer barricades, each towering titan supercharged by the lunar curse! Frontline knights are swatted across the dirt like dry leaves, their enchanted shields folding like tin foil beneath catastrophic club strikes! Chiffon turns ghost-white: a single Black Ogre nearly snapped his spine in two, but facing three blood-frenzied behemoths at once is certain death! Young Babel's legs turn to jelly as he drops behind his escort, while fat Count Rebrecca trips over his cape, squealing in undiluted dread. The alpha ogre raises a massive tree-trunk club to crush the command tent, when suddenly, the atmospheric pressure plummets to absolute zero! A deafening thunderclap splits the clouds as a piercing azure lightning spear arcs from the heavens at hypersonic velocity! Clad in crackling celestial plasma, our boy Joshua drops out of the sky like an enraged deity, his wooden spear radiating pure divine judgment! Piercing straight through the lead ogre's skull, tens of thousands of volts explode through the titan's nervous system, instantly cooking its brain! The five-ton behemoth drops like a lead weight, dead before its massive body even hits the ground, plunging the entire clearing into stunned silence! Standing proudly atop the monster's smoldering carcass, Joshua wipes a speck of ash from his cheek, looking completely bored by the spectacle. Chiffon rubs his eyes in complete existential crisis: killing a lunar-buffed Black Ogre in one single blow should require an entire B-rank knight company! Duke Aden watches quietly from the back, recognizing the pristine signature of elemental lightning infused directly into physical spear craft! Unable to accept that a lowborn child achieved what he couldn't, Rebrecca shrieks that it had to be a trick—Duke Aden must have struck from the shadows! Joshua shoots the fat count a sideways glance that could curdle milk, utterly disgusted by aristocrats who bark loudest when danger is furthest. But two more enraged ogres charge from the flanks, roaring for revenge as their iron morningstars whistle through the nocturnal gloom! Our boy calmly warns the trembling guards to fall back twenty paces unless they want their steel breastplates permanently welded to their flesh! Closing his eyes for a split second, Joshua synchronizes his heartbeat with the refined frequency of the Primordial Stone Bronto. He knows his nine-year-old vessel can only sustain one full-output burst, so he decides to eradicate every attacking beast in a single blast! Planting his boots into the soil, Joshua spins his weapon in a wide circle, unleashing the forbidden form: Magic Spear Technique, Thunderstorm! A cataclysmic dome of electrical scythes explodes across the camp, carving through ogre armor, flesh, and stone with surgical brutality! Both attacking behemoths are instantly vaporized into smoldering charcoal, sending shockwaves that blow the cloaks clean off the watching knights! Count Rebrecca hits his knees in the mud like a sack of grain, his jaw trembling as his entire worldview of noble superiority shatters to pieces. Veteran knights whisper in terror: wielding physical spear arts while freely manipulating elemental lightning is the mythical trademark of a Magic Swordsman! In the center of the crater, Joshua forces his chin up high. He knows that in the cutthroat Agnus family, showing even a hint of weakness is fatal. Watching from ten paces away, Duke Aden notices the micro-tremors in Joshua's hands, quietly respecting the iron will of his youngest boy. Court Archmage Arman steps forward, adjusting his spectacles with trembling fingers as he assesses the magical mechanics behind the devastation. Duke Aden quietly asks his court mage for an unfiltered assessment: how does Joshua compare against the prodigies of the continent? Arman gasps that in magic circles, Joshua is already performing above a third-class wizard, effortlessly pulling off the impossible miracle of double casting! Arman proclaims that Joshua isn't just talented—he is a generational monster who could reshape imperial history and fulfill Duke Aden's deepest ambitions! Aden nods slowly. For the first time in nine years, the iron duke sees the true future sovereign of House Agnus standing before him. But back in the clearing, the extreme backlash hits all at once. Joshua's vision blurs into darkness as his small body reaches its absolute physical limit. Seeing the boy stagger, a wounded Black Ogre rears up from the ash, lunging with a brutal downward hammer fist to crush him into paste! Before the beast's fist can travel an inch, a sonic boom shatters the air as Duke Aden blurs across the field, cleaving the ogre cleanly in two! Showered in evaporating monster blood, Duke Aden effortlessly catches his falling son, wrapping him securely against his armored chest. Feeling the warmth of his father's protective embrace, Joshua lets his weary eyes close, drifting to sleep with a confident, victorious smirk. With the northern horrors conquered and a prodigy awakened, the stage is set for the journey home. But what dark premonitions await Joshua in his dreams?
