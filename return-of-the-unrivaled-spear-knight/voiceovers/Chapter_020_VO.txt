=== CHAPTER 020 VOICEOVER SCRIPT ===
Total Shots: 54 | Words: 1226

--- [RECORDING VERSION: CONTINUOUS PROSE] ---
Carried through the smoking embers in his father's sturdy arms, our boy Joshua feels the heavy weight of exhaustion drag him down. For an old warrior who spent his entire previous life betrayed, unloved, and fighting alone, this rare feeling of safety feels almost surreal. He lets his guard down and slips into unconsciousness, but what begins as peaceful rest instantly twists into a waking apocalyptic nightmare! Desperate screams shatter the void as colossal stone spires topple into ash, trapped inhabitants pleading for help through choking plumes of black smoke! Floating above the devastation as a spirit, Joshua rubs his spectral eyes in shock—this isn't some random castle, it is the ancestral seat of House Agnus! He tries to convince himself it is just a nightmare, but the choking heat, the smell of burnt flesh, and the spiritual mana are terrifyingly real. Heavy metallic footsteps drag through the courtyard embers as a battered warrior in scorched red officer regalia stumbles through the inferno. When the firelight illuminates the knight's scarred face, Joshua's heart skips a beat: trudging through the ruins is an adult, battle-hardened Babel! The haughty young master of today is gone, replaced by a broken commander weeping helpless tears as his ducal legacy crumbles into smoking rubble. Doing the math on Babel's age, Joshua realizes this apocalyptic purge occurred roughly ten years in the future, during the climax of the imperial civil war! Absorbing the Primordial Stone of Storms has resonated with his regressed soul, ripping open a psychic portal into the unwritten future timeline! Out of nowhere, razor-sharp wind scythes slice through the smoke, shredding Babel's breastplate and hurling him violently against a marble pillar! Parting the storm clouds, a magnificent winged wind serpent glides into the courtyard, radiating supreme pressure that crushes the oxygen from the air! Joshua's phantom recoils in horror: that divine beast is Sylion, the legendary High-Rank Wind Spirit spoken of only in ancient summoner myths! Stepping off the spirit's crown is a strikingly gorgeous woman with braided amber hair, draped in embroidered robes that billow like autumn leaves. Joshua freezes in disbelief: that ruthless conqueror is none other than Anna Bell Grace, the adult persona of little Iceline from Count Rebrecca's estate! She looks down at the bleeding Babel and speaks, but her voice is completely masked by impenetrable silence magic, scrambling her words into ancient runes! Unable to hear the exchange, Joshua grits his teeth in frustration, desperately trying to read her lips to uncover the motive behind the dukedom's downfall. But a horrifying realization dawns upon him: Anna never acted independently—if she is here with a High Spirit, her enigmatic master must be lurking nearby! Clinging to his family pride, Babel staggers to his feet, screaming that a pack of traitors can never extinguish the eternal glory of House Agnus! But a spectral chimera lunges from the shadows with bone-crushing speed, snapping Babel's enchanted broadsword in half like a dry matchstick! From behind the blazing colonnade steps a tall, cloaked figure, exuding an aura of pure continental authority that suffocates the entire courtyard. Pulling down his hood with graceful ease, the man reveals a handsome face crowned by a sickening, mockingly superior smirk! Joshua's spirit violently shakes with pure hatred as he recognizes the continent's most ruthless mastermind: Draxia Bell Grace! His overwhelming surge of emotional shock fractures the psychic connection, detonating the prophetic vision into blinding white shards! Gasping for oxygen, our boy Joshua violently bolts upright in bed, clutching his chest as cold sweat drenching his bandages! Looking down, he realizes he is resting inside a VIP recovery chamber at Castle Loch, meticulously wrapped in high-grade healing salves. He clenches his fists to check his mana circuit—and what he feels brings a fierce smirk to his bruised lips. The chaotic lightning from Bronto didn't cripple him—it completely expanded his meridians, pushing his Magic Spear Arts directly into the second stage! While still officially a C-Rank knight, his elemental output and perfected technique allow him to easily dismantle veteran B-Rank masters with ease. A quiet voice suddenly cuts through the silence, as a familiar armored silhouette gazes out at the tranquil night sky from the window. Duke Aden sits motionless in an armchair, watching over his recovering son with the quiet vigilance of an apex predator. Aden remarks on how peacefully the stars shine now that the blood moon has passed, offering rare praise for Joshua's heroic stand. Shifting into business mode, the Duke lays out their immediate schedule: as soon as they reach the dukedom, Joshua will ride straight to the capital! Aden warns that walking into Arcadia as an official Agnus heir would paint a massive bullseye on his forehead for every scheming imperial faction. Between Fourth Prince Kaiser's secret police and rival ducal families, an acknowledged Agnus prodigy would be targeted for poison or blackmail immediately. To shield him from political sabotage, Aden has forged a complete false pedigree: Joshua will enroll as the sole orphan of the fallen Viscount Frederick! A ruined countryside noble striving to revive his dead estate will be dismissed as harmless fodder, giving Joshua total freedom to train undisturbed. Joshua nods without hesitation. Having lived through decades of palace treachery, he knows a low profile is the greatest tactical weapon of all. Operating under the radar means he can plunder ancient dungeons, recruit forgotten prodigies, and master Lugia without Emperor Marcus interfering! Duke Aden pushes himself up from his chair, preparing to let his son rest, but suddenly halts in his tracks, freezing the room's temperature once more. Turning back with an unreadable expression, the Duke's piercing eyes lock onto Joshua with the intensity of an interrogator hunting treason. Joshua feels a chill run down his spine, immediately realizing he must have muttered something catastrophic while trapped in his psychic nightmare. Aden steps closer to the bed, murmuring in a low, thunderous tone that while delirious with fever, Joshua uttered a very interesting name. Leaning over the mattress, Duke Aden speaks the forbidden name that once bathed half the empire in blood: Draxia Bell Grace! Joshua's pupils contract into needlepoints: in his fury during the vision, he shouted the mastermind's name at the top of his lungs! Aden's voice drops to a deadly whisper, demanding to know how a child raised in an isolated ducal barn could possibly know that classified imperial taboo! Pin-drop silence falls over the chamber. One wrong syllable could expose Joshua's regression, while a clumsy lie will insult the Duke's intellect. Forcing his breathing to stay even, our boy Joshua prepares to navigate the deadliest interrogation of his life against the iron duke himself! Because beyond this bedroom and past the northern mountains, the imperial capital of Arcadia awaits—a city of gold, glory, and ruthless assassinations! Back at the Agnus estate, Duchess Vanessa is already pouring venomous wine, believing Joshua will return home only to find his mother in a grave! In the palace, Fourth Prince Kaiser plots his ascension, completely unaware that his former weapon of slaughter has returned as his executioner! Wrapped safely within his spatial ring, the divine spear Lugia hums with anticipation, ready to taste the blood of empire traitors once more! Twenty chapters conquered, the first major trial overcome, and the path to total continental supremacy carved in stone. The legend of the Spear Knight has officially begun!
