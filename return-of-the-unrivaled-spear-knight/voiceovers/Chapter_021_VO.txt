=== CHAPTER 021 VOICEOVER SCRIPT ===
Total Shots: 54 | Words: 1360

--- [RECORDING VERSION: CONTINUOUS PROSE] ---
Right after dropping the most radioactive name in imperial history, the air in the bedroom freezes into pure liquid nitrogen. Our boy Joshua stares back with wide eyes, his mind frantically calculating how a nine-year-old stable boy could explain knowing a classified continental war criminal. For five agonizing seconds, father and son engage in a battle of wills, before Duke Aden exhales a heavy breath and decides to drop the interrogation. The Duke tells him to rest up: whatever secrets the boy holds can wait, because tomorrow morning the entire army marches back home to House Agnus. Collapsing back onto his pillows, Joshua lets out the longest sigh of his life. That was way too close—one slip of the tongue nearly blew his entire cover! But while our hero rests, beneath the silent stone towers of Castle Loch, a nest of vipers is already cooking up a toxic new scheme. Deep inside a secluded basement vault, fat Viscount Beeg is practically sweating through his velvet doublet, pacing back and forth in pure panic. Beeg demands to know if his sister, Duchess Vanessa, is aware that the concubine's son just stole the entire northern campaign right out from under Babel's nose! Nursing his bruised ribs, Chiffon confirms the Duchess already received the report—and to say she is losing her aristocratic mind would be an understatement. Beeg stammers in complete denial: Joshua spent nine years shoveling dung in the stables! How on earth does a peasant brat awaken world-class martial arts alone?! Even young Lord Babel, hailed across the empire as a generational prodigy, required royal instructors, legendary elixirs, and personal tutoring from the Duke to reach his level. Yet Joshua received zero pennies, zero tutors, and zero resources—and still managed to one-shot an enraged Black Ogre that nearly turned Chiffon into horse paste! Chiffon admits the entire garrison is in awe, with veteran centurions swearing the concubine's son is the second coming of an ancient Magic Swordsman. But Beeg sneers with theatrical disgust: Magic Swordsmen are fairy tales! In the real world, sudden explosive power only comes from one forbidden source. The corrupt viscount claims Joshua must have made an illicit blood contract with a high-ranking Demon from the nether realms to steal dark power! Chiffon scoffs at the theory: Duke Aden inspected Joshua's mana directly, and if there were even a speck of demonic taint, the Duke would have cleaved the boy in two on the spot. Beeg laughs at Chiffon's naivety: in high-stakes imperial politics, truth is irrelevant! All they need is a believable rumor to paint a target on the boy's head! The viscount smirks that Joshua is still just an immature child who wanted to show off. That flashy lightning display will become the very noose that hangs him! Beeg reveals his trump card: his own daughter, little Anna Bell Grace, is attending the Imperial Academy in Arcadia this term! With Anna serving as their inside spy, they can monitor Joshua's every move in the capital, making sure the concubine's son never threatens Babel's succession. Meanwhile, hundreds of miles to the south, warm sunshine glints off the majestic marble terraces of the main Agnus Ducal estate. Up on the royal balcony, Princess Cheryl—Lady Charles of the Pontier family—is leaning against the railing, looking thoroughly bored out of her mind. Her loyal retainer, old knight Cox, comes charging out onto the terrace like an excited bulldog, shouting that urgent news has arrived from the northern frontier! Panting for air, Cox reports that young Joshua successfully crushed the monster horde, and the entire dukedom is preparing an enormous victory banquet in his honor! But Cheryl barely reacts, letting out a sluggish sigh that leaves Cox scratching his bearded chin in utter bewilderment. Cox is shocked: usually, the princess is obsessing over Joshua! Why is she acting like a deflated balloon right when her favorite person returns? Cheryl's gaze turns somber as she reminds Cox of the brutal political reality that brought her to House Agnus in the first place. The legendary Mercenary King unexpectedly formed an alliance with the rival Cromwell faction, putting House Pontier under imminent threat of financial and military ruin! Her father sent her here as a political sacrificial pawn—her sole mission was to charm and marry Babel to drag Duke Aden's armies into their civil dispute! Cox softens his voice, admitting he knows Cheryl despises Babel's arrogant personality and cannot bear the thought of becoming a political trophy wife. Cox looks her in the eye: whenever she interacts with Joshua, her genuine smile returns. He urges her not to surrender her happiness for an ungrateful family! Cheryl flushes red down to her neck, stammering that Joshua was an insolent brat who told her to shut up and slammed a door directly in her face! But Cox merely chuckles, gently proposing that before Joshua departs for the capital, she should invite him to a private meal and speak from the heart. Pretending to be reluctant, Cheryl agrees to the meeting, secretly pleased that she will get a chance to see her mysterious spear knight once more! That very evening, night falls as dazzling fireworks detonate across the sky, painting the city in vibrant showers of ruby, gold, and sapphire! The central banquet hall is bursting at the seams with feast platters, roaring laughter, and endless casks of northern ale celebrating the expedition's victory! Frontline knights drink until their faces turn red, telling wild stories of young Lord Joshua descending from the sky to obliterate colossal monsters in a single blow! But while the party rages downstairs, the man of the hour is already slipping away into the quiet upper corridors, accompanied by a loyal knight officer. The knight chuckles that the guest of honor shouldn't sneak out so early, casually addressing our boy as "my liege" without missing a single beat. Joshua gives him a deadpan look, warning him that throwing around titles like "my liege" inside the ducal estate will have Duchess Vanessa's inquisitors hunting him by midnight! But the knight doesn't care. Having witnessed Joshua's peerless courage on the frozen frontline, he has already sworn his eternal sword to the young master! Joshua rubs his temple with a smirk: "Do I not get a vote in who pledges fealty to me?" The knight merely laughs, bowing with unshakable noble devotion. Wishing him a peaceful recovery, the loyal retainer departs, leaving Joshua alone in the grand hallway beneath the cold glow of the arched stained glass. Watching him leave, bitter memories from thirty years ago surge to the surface of Joshua's mind like acid, reopening scars that never truly healed. In his previous life, dozens of powerful knights and lords begged to serve under him. But our boy rejected every single offer without hesitation. He had vowed to be Kaiser's singular sword. He intentionally avoided building a personal faction, terrified that gathering followers would make Kaiser suspect him of treason! He sacrificed fame, power, and comrades just to protect Kaiser's fragile ego—and in the end, Kaiser still poisoned him like a stray dog in the dark! A sudden jolt of PTSD strikes his skull like a hammer. Draxia Bell Grace... that treacherous snake was standing right beside Kaiser, smiling as Joshua bled out! Gasping for air against a stone pillar, Joshua is suddenly interrupted by the quiet footfalls of the ducal head butler stepping out of the dark. The old servant inquires if the young master requires a doctor. Joshua shakes his head dismissively, telling the butler to go about his business. But the butler refuses to move, announcing in a flat, unyielding tone that a private dining table has already been prepared and everyone is waiting. Joshua frowns with annoyance, reminding the servant that he already declined the public banquet because of his battle fatigue and injuries. Leaning forward with a chilling glint in his eye, the butler drops the hammer: this isn't a public party—Duchess Vanessa has personally set a private table just for him! And the final poisoned hook? "Your mother Lucia has already arrived at the table." In a split second, Joshua's world turns to ice—Vanessa has taken his mother hostage!
