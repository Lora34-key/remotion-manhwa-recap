=== CHAPTER 026 VOICEOVER SCRIPT ===
Total Shots: 54 | Words: 1250

--- [RECORDING VERSION: CONTINUOUS PROSE] ---
Deep inside the lavish private parlor, black market kingpin Baron Provallum is sweating bullets as a cold voice reminds him his deadline just expired. Provallum stammers that procuring living elves is nearly impossible on short notice—they are legendary woodland recluses who don't just walk into slave cages! Lounging on the velvet sofa is Young Lord Veron Shen Villas, sneering that he pays exorbitant fees precisely so he never has to hear cheap whining. Veron rises to his feet, casually offering Provallum a single week to produce the captive elf, his icy tone hiding a razor-sharp threat. Swallowing his terror, Provallum forces a greasy smile, swearing on his life that his underground smugglers will deliver the specimen without fail. Veron strolls out with a parting warning: fail again, and Provallum won't live long enough to enjoy a single silver coin in this empire. The second the door clicks shut, the underground boss collapses into his chair, groaning that every meeting with Veron shaves years off his life. His clerk points out the terrifying truth: seven days isn't enough time to comb the southern frontiers, much less smuggle an elf past imperial checkpoints! Provallum grits his teeth: they have to make it happen, because if they fail, Veron will tear down his entire syndicate brick by bloody brick! Veron isn't just another spoiled brat; he is the sole heir of Marquis Villas, the supreme military commander holding Emperor Marcus’s absolute personal trust. With the imperial army in his family’s pocket, Veron has spent his entire fourteen years learning how to break people until they kiss his boots. As his luxury carriage rolls through downtown Arcadia, Veron's personal knight officer, Muker, asks if Provallum can truly deliver such a prize. Veron chuckles dismissively: of course the fat merchant will fail, but that was the entire point of setting an impossible deadline from the start! Muker looks puzzled: why demand an impossible task if you already know the contractor can't fulfill the contract in time? Veron leans forward, asking his knight what single weapon a monarch must master to keep commoners, nobles, and scoundrels thoroughly tamed. Muker tentatively suggests the classic philosophy of the carrot and the stick—rewarding loyalty while punishing disobedience. Veron laughs: carrots only work on starving animals, but Provallum is already a fat, greedy pig gorged on black market gold! To domesticate a fat pig, you break its legs with the whip; once it's paralyzed by mortal terror, you offer a tiny crumb of mercy. By the time the deadline runs out, Provallum will be so desperate that he will happily hand over his entire black market empire just to stay breathing. Muker shudders with dark admiration: a fourteen-year-old child manipulating veteran underworld barons like marionettes is truly terrifying. Feeling thoroughly satisfied with his scheming, Veron decides he's built up an appetite and orders the driver to find the most exclusive restaurant nearby. The carriage halts before an elite establishment boasting authentic cuisine prepared by retired master chefs from the imperial palace itself. Meanwhile, inside that very same dining hall, our boy Joshua and his loyal guard Sir Cain have been enjoying a quiet afternoon meal. Suddenly, a soft, familiar voice calls out across the tables: Count Poncel Jean Rebrecca and his lovely daughter have spotted them! Count Rebrecca bows with genuine modesty, apologizing if their sudden greeting interrupted Joshua and Cain's peaceful lunch. Sir Cain returns the bow immediately; among the cutthroat imperial nobility, Count Rebrecca is famous for treating even common footmen with honor. Joshua watches the Count quietly, recalling from his past life that Poncel was born with a sickly constitution and a heart far too kind for politics. High-ranking aristocrats openly mocked Poncel for refusing to exploit his peasants, calling him weak-willed and unworthy of his count's coronet. But Poncel never wavered; while his peers wallowed in corrupt luxury, his compassion made him the most revered nobleman among the empire's common people. Next to him, young Lady Iceline steps forward, her flowing sapphire hair shimmering as she offers our boy Joshua a delicate, shy curtsy. She softly admits that she recognized his midnight-blue hair the moment they walked in, fidgeting slightly as she struggles to find the right words. Joshua raises an eyebrow in amused surprise: in his past life, the legendary Spirit Master Anna Bell Grace was notorious for being colder than an iceberg! But their peaceful reunion is shattered as heavy armored footsteps echo through the archway, accompanied by a guard loudly demanding the hall be cleared. Veron Shen Villas steps inside, but freezes mid-sentence as his eyes lock straight onto Iceline, his arrogant scowl morphing into raw fascination. Veron looks her up and down with greasy entitlement, astonished that a maiden of such ethereal beauty was hiding out in a public restaurant. Striding directly into her personal space, Veron demands to know which provincial family she belongs to, his tone dripping with condescension. Iceline visibly recoils, her skin crawling from his shameless stare, while knight Muker steps forward barking that she should show proper manners! Muker loudly proclaims that the boy before them is Veron Shen Villas, the direct heir to the highest military authority outside the imperial family itself! Sir Cain's face pales: Marquis Villas is one of the empire's legendary Twelve Pillars—why on earth is his notorious son parading around down here?! Seeing his daughter cornered, Count Rebrecca steps between them like a shield, bowing smoothly to diffuse the dangerous tension before blood is drawn. Veron doesn't even bother returning the greeting, glancing down at the frail count with blatant contempt as if an insect just blocked his view. With unflinching dignity, Poncel introduces himself as the head of the Rebrecca family, hoping his status as a sitting count will restore order. But Veron just scoffs: he doesn't give a damn about the father; he claims the daughter committed an unforgivable insult by daring to look away from him! Cain grits his teeth in silent fury: etiquette doesn't require formal obeisance in a tavern, but Veron is flexing his father's rank purely to break the girl. Veron shoves past the Count, reaching out toward Iceline while screaming that she will kneel and apologize before he loses his patience completely! Just as Veron’s hand reaches out, our boy Joshua steps out with hands in his pockets, his calm voice cutting through the hall like a winter frost. Joshua casually drops the ultimate verbal nuke: Marquis Villas must be having sleepless nights knowing his sole heir is an absolute, irredeemable idiot! The entire restaurant goes dead silent before Veron shrieks in disbelief, his aristocratic pride shattering into unhinged homicidal rage! Muker draws his steel blade with a deafening screech, lightning mana crackling down the edge as he screams that the insolent brat has a death wish! Facing a drawn sword, Joshua doesn't even blink, calmly reminding the knight that drawing steel on a noble carries lethal consequences. Joshua’s eyes flare with suffocating red aura: since the knight drew his weapon first, whatever happens next is entirely on his own head! Muker freezes, his sword arm trembling violently as a mountain of ancient slaughterhouse pressure bears down on his skull—this isn't a child, it's a monster! As Veron screams for his paralyzed guard to strike, Joshua smoothly reveals his covert alias: he is Ash von Frederick of the fallen southern nobility! With a predatory smile, our boy Joshua steps forward, promising that starting right now, he's going to engrave that name into their souls forever!
