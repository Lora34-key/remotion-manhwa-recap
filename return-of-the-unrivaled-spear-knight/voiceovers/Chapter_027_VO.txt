=== CHAPTER 027 VOICEOVER SCRIPT ===
Total Shots: 54 | Words: 1243

--- [RECORDING VERSION: CONTINUOUS PROSE] ---
While our boy Joshua prepares to throw hands in the capital, hundreds of miles away, Castle Loch rests under a cold, foreboding moonlight. Inside Viscount Vig's locked private chamber, the fat lord stares blankly while a hooded shadow agent named Ju-ang bows to the room. Seated across the table is young Anna Bell Grace, smiling sweetly as if discussing the afternoon weather rather than high-treason shadow plots. Ju-ang asks what emergency forced an urgent summons, noting that keeping deep-cover operatives moving in broad daylight risks imperial exposure. Anna cheerfully informs him that an unprecedented monster has emerged inside House Agnus, threatening to flip the continent's balance upside down. Ju-ang assumes she means Babel, the ducal heir who became a mana master at fourteen and was hailed as the empire's greatest prodigy. Anna shakes her head: Duke Aden had a secret second son whose terrifying combat genius makes Babel look like a clumsy amateur! Ju-ang recoils in sheer disbelief: Babel was pampered with sovereign elixirs from infancy; how could some hidden child surpass him before age ten?! Anna explains the harsh political reality: the masses celebrate decades of peace, but beneath the surface, rival empires have amassed colossal armies. When military power reaches its absolute saturation point, rulers inevitably look for an excuse to unleash it—meaning continental war is inevitable. Ju-ang warns that an unpredicted genius under Duke Aden could completely shatter their master plan before the first battle even starts. Anna smirks, revealing her master countermove: she will enroll directly in the Imperial Academy of Avalon to seduce or bend Joshua to their cause! But right now in Arcadia, our boy Joshua couldn't care less about distant academy plots—he has an arrogant marquis heir to humble! Veron mocks the name Frederick, sneering that Joshua must be the spawn of some ruined provincial hicks trying to play aristocrat in the big city. Joshua calmly asks if a surname really matters when one of them is about to get their teeth rearranged across the dining room floor. Veron loses his mind: how dare a backwater insect mock House Villas? He vows to eradicate Joshua's entire lineage down to the pets! Veron demands Joshua drop to his knees and beg, claiming he might show mercy and only chop off a single arm or leg! Our boy Joshua doesn't even bother with noble banter—he simply looks the spoiled brat in the eye and calls complete and utter bullshit! Veron chokes on his own spit in absolute outrage, shrieking for knight Muker to shatter the brat's kneecaps and force him into the dirt! Shaking off the brief chill of Joshua's killing intent, Muker roars his obedience, raising his broadsword to execute the impudent child. Muker launches forward with lethal speed, igniting his blade with torrents of cyan lightning mana to end the fight in a single strike! The veteran knight barks that Joshua signed his own death warrant the moment he dared insult the bloodline of the Imperial Marquis! But Joshua doesn't draw a spear or evade—he casually raises his bare arm, condensing pure primordial mana around his knuckles like iron plate! Clang! The lightning-wrapped blade crashes into Joshua's bare fist, grinding to a dead halt with the deafening ringing of an anvil! Muker's jaw hits the floor: a nine-year-old child just caught a full-power mana blade with his bare knuckles without a single scratch! Before the knight can pull back, Joshua slips inside his guard and buries a thunderous, mana-packed straight punch directly into his solar plexus! An explosive shockwave erupts through Muker's spine, caving in his armor and rupturing his internal mana channels in a split second! The certified knight folds like a cheap lawn chair, crashing face-down onto the tiles in a pool of his own blood, completely out cold! Veron stumbles backward into a table, his mind short-circuiting: how on earth could a pint-sized kid flatten a mana-wielding knight in one punch?! Joshua casually dusts off his sleeve and turns toward Veron with a sinister smile, asking how the young master would like his lesson delivered. Shaking with terror, Veron tries to hide behind his usual arrogance, shrieking that touching a single hair on his head will bring down the imperial army! Step by steady step, Joshua closes the distance, calmly repeating that his name is Ash von Frederick—and he doesn't like repeating himself. Veron screams at the top of his lungs, reminding everyone that he is the sole heir of House Villas and that nobodies like Frederick are mere worms— BAM! Joshua doesn't let him finish the sentence, detonating a devastating left hook straight across Veron's obnoxious jawline! Veron goes airborne in slow motion, spitting broken teeth and blood before crashing through mahogany tables and slamming hard into the back wall! Joshua casually strolls over, standing above the whimpering heir and telling him to wake up, because class has only just begun! Joshua lifts Veron by his silk collar with one hand, mocking him for crying like a baby the second his family guards aren't around to protect him. As Veron’s eyes flutter shut in terror, Joshua winds up a second punch, cheerily telling the brat he's not allowed to faint until the debt is paid! Just before Joshua’s fist caves in Veron's skull, a steel blade slams into the floor tiles between them as Muker screams for him to stop! Bleeding from his mouth, Muker drags his shattered body onto one knee, begging for mercy and admitting they committed unforgivable disrespect! Muker slams his forehead directly against the marble floor, offering his own sword arm as a sacrifice if Joshua will only spare his young master! Joshua pauses, recognizing the profound weight of a knight throwing away his sacred pride and risking permanent mutilation just to shield his lord. The sight triggers a painful memory of his past life—a loyal subordinate who threw away his own future and bled out just to buy Joshua time. Joshua sighs, his killing intent receding: he refuses to become a monster who ignores genuine, selfless knightly loyalty. But our boy doesn't let them walk away for free—he smoothly slips the ornate Villas family signet ring right off Veron's limp finger! Tossing the signet ring in his hand, Joshua tells Muker that if Veron ever wants his ducal ring back, he will have to come find him in person! And if the spoiled marquis heir is too cowardly to face him, Joshua will happily auction the sacred Villas crest on the black market for pure gold! Turning on his heel, Joshua walks back to his table where Sir Cain is massaging his temples, wondering how a simple lunch turned into grand larceny! Cain mutters that while Joshua's charisma is unmatched, extorting the sole heir of the empire's greatest military titan is borderline suicidal! Joshua smirks, warning Cain never to copy his style unless he wants Duke Aden to personally throw him into a dungeon for reckless behavior! Watching Joshua stride away, Count Poncel Rebrecca shakes his head in absolute reverence: that boy carries the aura of a sovereign emperor. But young Lady Iceline isn't about to let him slip away—she gathers up her dark blue gown and runs down the marble staircase right after him! The poor count reaches out in utter confusion, left behind with a shattered restaurant and two battered imperial aristocrats on the floor! Sighing with his trademark gentle heart, Count Rebrecca awkwardly offers to escort the groaning Villas retinue to the nearest hospital!
