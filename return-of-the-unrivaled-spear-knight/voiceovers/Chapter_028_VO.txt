=== CHAPTER 028 VOICEOVER SCRIPT ===
Total Shots: 54 | Words: 1188

--- [RECORDING VERSION: CONTINUOUS PROSE] ---
High inside the opulent recovery hall of Shani Temple, golden sunlight streams past stained glass as divine priests perform emergency holy healing. Young Lord Veron groggily opens his eyes, finding himself in the empire's most expensive temple clinic where holy magic costs a literal fortune. Stumbling over to the gilded mirror, Veron catches his reflection and lets out a blood-curdling screech that rattles the temple rafters! His prized handsome smile is completely ruined—our boy Joshua's fist knocked his front teeth clean out of his gums! Veins bulging on his forehead, Veron barks for Muker to drag the brat back immediately so he can personally carve him to pieces! But Muker bows his head in grim humiliation, stating bluntly that with his current abilities, defeating that demonic child is completely impossible. Veron violently kicks his knight's shins, screaming that the entire Villas escort retinue will face brutal military torture once they return home! Veron vows to deploy private legions to locate House Frederick and slit the throats of Joshua's parents, siblings, and servants right in front of him! Muker stops him cold: they have zero justification. If they deploy troops over a restaurant brawl, the political fallout will destroy them! No noble in the capital will believe the elite guard of House Villas was crushed by a ten-year-old child—and if they do, their prestige collapses instantly! Muker warns that for great houses, public honor is worth more than life itself; if Marquis Villas finds out, his wrath won't stop at simple discipline! Remembering his ruthless father, Veron shivers, then suggests their usual cleanup: massacre everyone present in the restaurant and bury the bodies! Muker drops the devastating truth: they can't cover it up, because undeniable physical evidence was stolen from the crime scene! Ash von Frederick didn't just walk away victorious—he confiscated the supreme signet token of House Villas right off Veron's hand! Veron frantically pats his coat and fingers, gasping in horror as he realizes the sacred family heirloom is completely missing! That ring wasn't just a signet—it was the Deon Ring, a B-Class national treasure forged by the Magic Tower worth more than an entire castle! Muker explains that Joshua told Veron to come beg in person if he ever wants his family's sacred artifact returned in one piece. Realizing Joshua calculated every single move in advance, Muker advises sending a reliable proxy who won't leak their humiliating defeat. Swallowing his shattered pride, Veron agrees: they need someone influential enough to speak for them, but expendable enough to keep quiet. Back at the dining table, our boy Joshua casually twirls the heavy golden Deon Ring, admiring its intricate sword-and-shield engravings. Sir Cain leans forward nervously, asking if Joshua is truly certain the marquis's spoiled heir will come looking for his stolen treasure. Joshua smirks: Veron can't afford to lose a Magic Tower artifact, but his fragile pride will prevent him from coming in person! He predicts Veron will dispatch his personal shadow proxy—someone of high underground status who answers directly to House Villas! In Arcadia's underworld, Baron Provallum runs the black market night, but House Villas controls Provallum—meaning the baron himself will soon arrive! Cain's mind reels: he assumed Joshua picked a fight out of childish temper, but our boy orchestrated the entire brawl to reel in Provallum! Cain grips the table in silent awe, realizing that the nine-year-old before him possesses political foresight that rivals veteran imperial grand dukes! Just as they finish their tactical analysis, a soft, nervous voice interrupts: Lady Iceline has returned, fidgeting shyly with her dark blue gown. Bowing with pure sincerity, she thanks him for defending her father's honor and shielding their family from Veron's vicious humiliation. Joshua casually waves it off, telling her not to overthink it; he had his own tactical reasons for rearranging that spoiled brat's face. Joshua studies her with genuine curiosity: how on earth does this gentle, sweet-natured girl eventually become the cold-blooded Grand Sorceress of Ice? In his previous life, Iceline was an emotionless queen of frost, wielding unique ice magic that slaughtered armies without shedding a single tear. Snapping back to the present, Iceline vows that if House Villas seeks revenge, House Rebrecca will testify before the Emperor to clear his name! Joshua chuckles: imperial politics isn't solved by honest testimony. But if she truly feels indebted, he has a much better proposition! Joshua asks her for a single personal favor in the future—locking in a future debt from the continent's greatest upcoming archmage! Delighted by the simple request, Iceline beams and promises on her life that she will do anything he asks, blushing as she takes her leave! As she disappears into the drizzling rain, Joshua watches through the window, his smile turning sharp as his sixth sense tingles. Outside in the pouring downpour, a cloaked figure crouches motionless on the rooftop across the avenue, monitoring every breath inside the restaurant. It is Zero, the supreme branch director of the Moon Door, sweating profusely as he recalls Joshua looking straight into his eyes through the rain! Zero’s stealth arts were perfected to fool Master-rank swordsmen, yet Joshua spotted him instantly and smirked like a predator toying with prey! Zero realizes Joshua's knowledge of the Moon Door isn't lucky gossip—this boy is an unfathomable entity who must be reported to guild leadership! Right as the shadow informant vanishes, a private carriage pulls up, and a stout, bald aristocrat steps out into the rain. It is Baron Accent Provallum, the ruler of Arcadia's black market, arriving in person as predicted by our boy Joshua! Joshua greets him smoothly by name, noting that the great underworld baron arrived far faster than expected! Though Provallum commands wealth that rivals high dukes, he bows with deep courtesy before the supposed heir of House Frederick. Sir Cain is dumbfounded: Provallum has the military backing of Marquis Villas, yet he is groveling before a child from the countryside! Provallum gets straight to the point: he is here as Veron's representative to reclaim the precious Deon Ring and settle the dispute peacefully. Joshua coldly rejects him: he told Veron to come in person, and if the coward refuses, Joshua will simply melt the priceless artifact into scrap! Provallum’s temples throb with fury, stammering that Veron cannot walk because our boy literally knocked his front teeth into the back of his throat! Joshua sighs at the heir's pathetic fragility, conceding that if Veron is too frail to walk, Provallum must bring knight Muker along next time. Relieved, Provallum asks if that means Joshua will finally negotiate the return of the ring once Muker arrives to represent the family. Joshua nods, but adds that business is a two-way street—since he agreed to entertain their request, the baron must grant him a small favor first! Joshua points directly at Provallum's fat fingers, cheerfully remarking that his diamond-encrusted gold rings look exceptionally valuable! The black market boss freezes in utter disbelief: this nine-year-old kid is shamelessly extorting the underworld emperor of Arcadia! Beckoning with a cheeky smile, our boy Joshua orders the underworld tycoon to cough up his jewelry right now—talk about peak daylight robbery!
