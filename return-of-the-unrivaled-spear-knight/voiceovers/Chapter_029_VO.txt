=== CHAPTER 029 VOICEOVER SCRIPT ===
Total Shots: 54 | Words: 1220

--- [RECORDING VERSION: CONTINUOUS PROSE] ---
Walking back beneath the capital streetlamps, Sir Cain chuckles that his young master has officially transformed into a ruthless demon! Cain can't wrap his head around how a nine-year-old child just casually stripped the supreme ruler of Arcadia's underworld of his personal jewelry! Joshua shrugs: he didn't rob anyone—giving that corrupt baron a second chance to breathe is a luxury that demands a hefty consultation fee! Raising the ring into the moonlight, Joshua reveals the truth: this unassuming piece of jewelry is the legendary heirloom known as the Elf's Tears. Centuries ago, greedy human nobles hunted the ethereal elven race for their beauty, capturing them into chains and slaughtering their sacred forests. Driven to the southern edge of the world, high elven mages combined their lifeforce to weave a colossal, impenetrable continental barrier around their forest. Dozens of High Elves perished maintaining the barrier, leaving behind a single surviving sovereign: Elunbug, the immortal High King of the Elves! For hundreds of years, only one single human was ever permitted to enter the sacred forest: Xerath Dick Orvis, the sworn friend of King Elunbug. But human greed knew no bounds—imperial nobles destroyed House Orvis and tortured Xerath to his dying breath, yet he took the secret to his grave. Arriving too late, King Elunbug found Xerath's orphaned daughter living as a common servant; weeping in sorrow, his divine tears crystallized inside this emerald ring. That ring became known as the Elf's Tears, an ancient artifact that holds dormant powers far beyond anything modern magicians can replicate. Snapping out of his historical musings, Joshua halts outside the Agnus capital estate, turning toward Sir Cain with a solemn expression. Joshua addresses him directly: he needs to ask a serious favor that concerns not just his own future, but Cain's entire path as a knight. Cain salutes enthusiastically, ready to tear through dragons, but Joshua clarifies that this mission requires absolute discipline and patience. Joshua states coldly that he has zero use for comrades who get slaughtered before him; he needs sworn warriors he can trust with his back! Our boy reveals his plan: he is enrolling in the Avalon Imperial Academy alone, meaning Cain must relocate to protect his mother, Lady Lucia. Cain protests frantically: without an attending knight, Joshua will enter the academy as a penniless country noble with zero protection! In that viper's nest of high aristocracy, nobles will openly torment a lone commoner—and Veron Shen Villas will be waiting with his private academy gang! Joshua offers a bittersweet smile: after fighting through forty years of bloody civil war in his past life, surviving alone is his absolute specialty! Then Joshua lowers his head with total sincerity, asking Cain not as a liege, but as a brother, to master Duke Aden's legendary sword arts! Cain's heart shatters: seeing the sovereign he worships bow before him is unbearable—he rushes forward, begging Joshua to lift his head! Cain accepts the duty under one condition: if business is an equal exchange, then Joshua must grant his knight a personal vow in return. With fierce tears in his eyes, Cain asks Joshua to become an emperor so absolute that Cain will never have to kneel before another soul in existence! Joshua smirks with absolute majesty, swearing upon his very soul that Sir Cain will never bow his head to anyone other than him! Meanwhile, thousands of leagues away at the geographical core of the continent, an ivory spire pierces the heavens—the legendary Magic Tower! Buffered by three rival superpowers, the Magic Tower stands as an untouchable sovereign nation, holding the supreme pride and lore of all mages. At the pinnacle of the citadel sits the Grand Council Chamber, where the Seven Magicians convene to decide the fate of continental magic. Presiding over the council is Iain Jean Perry, the world's first living 9th-Class Archmage and supreme Master of the Magic Tower! Iain scowls at three empty seats: Flame and Void wander the world doing whatever they please, while Thunder abruptly vanished yesterday! Marcus of the Earth Seat takes a calm sip of tea, explaining that Thunder rushed off to investigate an unprecedented emergency in Avalon. Iain cuts through the chatter: today's 27th regular conference has only one urgent agenda—the awakening of the legendary Primordial Stones! Theta of the Storm Seat smirks, remarking that it's unlike the old man to skip diplomatic formalities and dive straight to the punchline. Marcus reprimands the young archmage to show proper decorum, while Theta playfully mocks him as "Mister Earth" before falling silent. Dropping his playful facade, Theta asks if the chilling rumors are true—has someone actually discovered and claimed Magma, the Stone of Fire?! Marcus drops an even bigger shocker: not only has Magma been taken, but Bronto, the Primordial Stone of Storms, has completely vanished! Theta is dumbfounded: Primordial Stones are mythological entities sealed since the creation of the world—how could two vanish simultaneously?! Speaking for the first time, Lady Elisha of the Ice Seat warns that only the Magic Tower possessed the ancient coordinates of the stones! If an outsider stumbled upon them blindly, it's a minor nuisance; but if someone deliberately hunted them down, the continent faces catastrophe! Iain admits he already knows who took Magma, but Bronto vanished without a trace—no one knows whether it dissolved or was absorbed! Theta connects the dots: the Thunder Seat bolted for the Avalon Empire precisely to chase down the lingering lightning fallout of Bronto! Marcus drops the critical lead: right around the frozen crater where Bronto manifested, witnesses reported the appearance of a Magic Swordsman! Hearing the words "Magic Swordsman", Elisha kicks her throne back in absolute hysteria, gasping whether THAT legendary being has returned! Marcus shakes his head: it's not the ancient mythical founder; the magic swordsman reported in Avalon is far, far more unbelievable! Marcus drops the age that shatters all magical sanity: the monster who unleashed dual-cast lightning magic is a child barely nine years old! The council room explodes: forming a single circle before puberty is impossible, let alone dual-casting 3rd-class offensive magic into a blade! Elisha insists the boy must be the sole direct inheritor of the legendary Magic Swordsman, demanding the tower deploy investigators immediately! Marcus calmly adds the final piece of the puzzle: the child is unrelated to their founder—he is Joshua, the second son of Duke Aden von Agnus! Theta screams that Marcus should have opened the meeting with that headline instead of letting them stew in existential dread! BOOM! The 9th-Class Master slams the table with mana, commanding total silence and ordering every archmage to refrain from rash interference! Iain orders them to observe the Agnus Duchy strictly from the shadows; until the truth of Bronto is verified, no mage makes an open move! Grudgingly accepting the Master's decree, the archmages bow and exit, their minds spinning with the impossible name: Joshua von Agnus. Left alone in the cavernous council hall, the continent's strongest living wizard stares out into the moonlit night sky with heavy eyes. Iain murmurs to the empty room: the world is shifting with dizzying speed—and whether this child brings salvation or doom remains to be seen! As the Seven Magicians turn their gaze toward Avalon, our boy Joshua has just ignited the fuse of a global storm that will shake the empire to its core!
