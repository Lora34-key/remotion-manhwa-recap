=== CHAPTER 030 VOICEOVER SCRIPT ===
Total Shots: 54 | Words: 1196

--- [RECORDING VERSION: CONTINUOUS PROSE] ---
Early the next morning inside the private courtyard, dawn breaks across the marble tiles as our boy Joshua prepares to awaken the Orvis relic. The emerald ring hovers between his fingertips, vibrating violently as ancient golden glyphs peel away from its polished surface! Closing his eyes, Joshua recites forgotten primordial runes—not modern magic circles, but the original lost language of the world! Sir Cain covers his eyes in absolute shock: runes are the sacred domain of 5th-Class archmages—how is a nine-year-old chanting them fluently?! With a final flash of golden stardust, the seal shatters and the ring drops into Joshua's palm, pulsing with tranquil elven energy. Cain rushes forward to check on him, gasping to know what kind of divine blessing was engraved into that defiled black market treasure. Joshua explains that the ring automatically deploys a sovereign 7th-Class barrier in life-threatening danger, while boosting natural affinity with spirits! Dropping to one knee, Cain asks if he can bear the ring into battle, hoping its immense protection will let him shield Joshua from harm. Joshua chuckles: the ring was woven exclusively for Xerath's daughter, meaning men cannot channel its magical matrix even if they try! Our boy’s eyes soften: he hunted down this relic for one reason only—to slip it onto his beloved mother Lucia's finger, keeping her safe forever. Meanwhile, deep within an underground sanctum in Arcadia, Moon Door branch director Zero activates a classified communications orb. A shimmering projection materializes: the legendary Grandmaster of the Moon Door, the supreme phantom who commands the continent's underworld! Zero bows low, reporting that Joshua von Agnus isn't just an anomaly—he is hiding combat abilities and classified knowledge that defy all reason. The Grandmaster responds with chilling calm: he already knows the child is extraordinary, but the Magic Tower has officially mobilized to find him! Zero’s jaw drops: the isolationist Magic Tower never interferes in empire politics—why would continental archmages deploy over a ducal bastard?! The Grandmaster explains: the Seven Magicians believe Joshua is the secret blood descendant of Ainslott, the legendary founder of magic swordsmanship! The Primordial Stones are merely a pretext—the Imperial Knights, the Magic Tower, and their own guild's sacred Red Coin are all converging on Joshua! The Grandmaster orders Zero to secure Joshua's trust at all costs: whoever controls this boy will control the destiny of the entire continent! Zero salutes as the transmission terminates, his resolve hardening to tether his shadow network to our boy Joshua's meteoric rise. But hundreds of miles to the north, a violent tempest tears through the ancient wilderness as roaring thunder splits the dark night sky! Tied to a dead tree trunk in the freezing rain is a bruised, bound aristocrat—gasping and squirming in pure, blind terror! Approaching him through the mud is a hooded mage crackling with raw electrical discharge, ordering his captive to shut up and watch! Crashing through the treeline comes a feral horde of giant ogres, their blood-red eyes locked onto the solitary wizard standing in their path! Without taking a step back, the mage spreads his arms wide, chanting an ancient high-tier incantation that vibrates the entire forest! The Roman numeral VII glows beneath his eye, confirming his identity as a legendary pillar of the Magic Tower: the Thunder Seat! Calling down the wrath of the heavens, he unleashes his signature destruction spell: **Lightning Rain**! A biblical deluge of lightning shafts smites the forest floor, obliterating the ogre horde into smoldering ash in less than two seconds! The forest falls dead silent, save for the hissing of vaporized monster flesh as the archmage strolls calmly back toward his captive. Ripping the gag away, the mage demands to know how his lightning magic stacks up against the combat power of young Joshua von Agnus! Gasping for breath, the captive is revealed: it's Viscount Vig, the cowardly uncle who tried to assassinate Joshua at the banquet dinner! Vig shrieks that he is an imperial peer and that Duke Aden and the Emperor will hunt the mage down to the ends of the earth! The archmage chuckles: if he feared the Avalon Empire, he never would have crossed the border in the first place—so start talking! Sweating bullets, Vig flatters him frantically, swearing that the archmage's lightning is a thousand times stronger than that brat's parlor tricks! The mage nods: Ainslott never wielded lightning, meaning Joshua must have absorbed Bronto, the Primordial Stone of Storms, just as he suspected! He resolves to confront Joshua personally, but sighs that breaking into Duke Aden's ancestral fortress is nearly impossible even for him. Seeing a chance to save his miserable skin, Vig interrupts: if he wants Joshua, searching Castle Loch is a complete waste of time! Vig eagerly sells out his nephew: Joshua went to Arcadia with Duke Aden and has already entered the prestigious Imperial Academy! The archmage scowls: Arcadia is crawling with Emperor Marcus's elite guard and monster masters, making a quiet assassination tricky. Desperate to crush Joshua, Vig offers his underground noble connections, promising to smuggle the archmage right into the heart of Arcadia! The archmage chuckles condescendingly: what could an overweight, cowardly provincial noble possibly offer a supreme magician? Vig boasts his aristocratic knowledge: pointing out the Steropes brand, he correctly identifies the 6th-Class Thunder Seat of the Magic Tower! He screams out the legendary name: **Jack Steropes**, one of the Seven Magicians who reign over the mortal realm! Jack Steropes blinks in mild surprise—a backwater swine actually recognized his name without having to be told! Convinced he's saved, Vig smirks, claiming that with his political network and Jack's magical firepower, Joshua will be crushed into paste! But Jack merely shrugs: no matter how urgent the task, an archmage of the Magic Tower doesn't hold hands with a corrupt, pathetic pig! Jack's face twists into a terrifying shadow: leaving witnesses alive who know his true identity is a rookie mistake he never makes! Vig’s soul leaves his body as he shrieks in horror: killing a sitting imperial viscount will bring down the full wrath of Duke Aden von Agnus! BOOM! A colossal bolt of pure thunder erupts from the sky, vaporizing the traitorous viscount and his excuses into microscopic dust! Turning his back on the smoldering crater, Jack smirks pitilessly: who on earth would waste five seconds searching for a worthless scumbag like him? With his target confirmed, the Thunder Seat sets his sights on the capital, hungry to test the mysterious child holding the power of Bronto! The storm is racing toward Arcadia—and the peaceful days our boy Joshua hoped for in the capital are officially dead on arrival! From a discarded stable boy left to die in the mud, our boy Joshua has conquered his first trial, unlocked an ancient elven relic, and set the entire continent on edge. With a 6th-Class Thunder Archmage marching on the capital, did Joshua make a fatal mistake sending Sir Cain away? Drop your theories in the comments below! That wraps up Part 1!
