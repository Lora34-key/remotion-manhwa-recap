Waking up face-down in a pile of damp hay surrounded by the pungent perfume of fresh horse manure is definitely not on anyone's reincarnation bingo card. Joshua snaps his eyes open with an excruciating migraine that feels like a dwarven blacksmith hammering white-hot iron directly against his skull. In his previous life as the supreme champion of the empire, he fell asleep under royal chandeliers and woke up in silk sheets imported from the eastern continent. But right now? Cobwebs, rusty nails, and literal stable compost. Yet the longer he breathes in the dust, the faster a chilling sense of dread crawls up his spine. He jumps to his feet and sprints like a madman toward the stable entrance, throwing both hands forward and slamming the heavy oak doors wide open. Crisp morning sunlight blinds him for a split second before revealing an enormous estate that stretches as far as the eye can see. And right there, blanketing the palace grounds, are acres of legendary blue roses. Across the entire empire, only one royal house cultivates blue roses: the Duchy of Agnus. Joshua rushes over to a puddle on the cobblestone path to check the water's reflection. Gone is the scarred, battle-hardened veteran with bleached gray hair. Staring right back at him is a scrawny ten-year-old boy with dark blue locks, completely smooth skin, and piercing, abyss-like sapphire eyes. He didn't just survive the emperor's betrayal—he regressed over forty whole years into the past! And you want to know what this absolute menace does first? He doesn't shed a tear, he doesn't panic—our boy throws his head back into the sky and lets out the most unhinged villain laugh you have ever heard! Because think about the mathematical advantage here: Kaiser Van Britten isn't an untouchable emperor sitting atop an imperial fortress. Right now, that snake is just an insecure ten-year-old fourth prince getting scolded by royal tutors. The chessboard just reset, and Joshua has the first move. But before he can draft a twenty-year plan to dismantle the imperial bloodline, three local rent-a-cops show up to ruin the mood. Enter Rols, Roid, and Gort—three bottom-tier centurions who made it their personal life mission to make Joshua's childhood a living hell. In high noble estates, regular guards would never dare lay a finger on ducal blood. But Joshua was an illegitimate bastard born from Duke Aden forcefully bedding a maid named Lucia. To the Duke, Joshua was nothing more than an embarrassing byproduct of a drunken night. He gave them a barn stall to live in and never bothered looking back. Seeing that the boy had zero protection, these three centurions derived a sick, twisted pleasure from bullying someone carrying actual ducal blood. Gort barks at him for slacking off, while Roid laughs that the little trash can't even speak—because throughout his childhood, Joshua stayed dead silent to survive. But then Rols and Roid cross the line into absolute suicide territory. They start giggling about Duchess Vanessa's private guarantee. Bragging that the Duchess herself promised them complete legal immunity, even if they violated his mother Lucia and threw Joshua's corpse in a ditch. And right there, something inside Joshua's soul snaps. He originally planned to lie low, avoid drawing attention, and build an army in the shadows. But you do not talk about his mother. Lucia was the only pure light in his miserable early life, and these walking corpses just signed their death warrants. To understand what makes our boy ridiculous, look at how magic works in this realm. An average knight meditates five grueling years just to construct a mana hall below their navel. Anyone who can manipulate mana by age fifteen is celebrated across the continent as a generational genius whose name gets carved into kingdom history. But the ancient technique Joshua unsealed alongside Lugia operates on completely inverted logic. It doesn't accumulate mana in a stomach vessel—it draws mana directly through the flesh! It bypasses the five-year mana hall grind entirely. In his first life, it took Joshua ten painful years to awaken his first handful of energy because nobody taught him how. But today? The grandmaster's soul already knows every spiritual junction by heart. He just needs to coax a single handful of atmospheric mana into his knuckles. Thinking the mute kid is paralyzed with fright, Gort rolls his shoulders forward, complaining that he got rejected by a girl and needs to blow off some steam. He raises a heavy wooden club high into the morning air, fully intending to fracture the defenseless ten-year-old's ribs for cheap amusement. But right as the club rips downward at lethal velocity, Joshua's eyelids snap open with a brilliant flash of azure lightning. With veteran muscle memory that laughs at basic club swings, Joshua slips the descending weapon by a fraction of a single hair's breadth. He pivots his back foot, ignites raw pale blue mana around his tiny knuckles, and plants a counter-punch directly into Gort's exposed stomach. The sound that reverberates across the stables is like wet leather ripping in two. Gort can't even manage a groan before his two-hundred-pound frame collapses limp into the mud. While the two guards stand frozen in shock, Joshua suddenly feels something terribly wrong. A sticky, repulsive knot pulses inside the pit of his stomach. His ten-year-old body has never circulated energy before. His meridians are clogged, burning like molten lead. He calculates he has enough power for exactly one more strike. Tactically, Rols is a calculating rat who relies on his eyes rather than his blade. If Joshua takes down Roid, Rols will never have the courage to charge. Furious that a toddler knocked out his partner, Roid unsheathes his iron longsword with a screeching clang, shouting that he's going to carve the brat into pieces. The blade whistles in a lethal horizontal arc aimed directly at Joshua's throat. But our boy bends his spine backward like a coiled steel spring. The steel breezes right past his eyelashes. As Roid overextends his swing, he looks down and sees the boy's fist igniting in pure sapphire flame. Joshua snaps back upward with vicious momentum, driving an absolute textbook hundred-point uppercut directly into Roid's jaw! A brutal crunch echoes across the courtyard as Roid's jaw shatters on impact, launching his airborne body backward like a ragdoll. Two seasoned imperial centurions, both flattened within thirty seconds by an unarmed ten-year-old child who was supposedly mute. Rols stumbles backward in absolute panic. He realizes that knocking out Gort wasn't some lucky fluke—this child has been concealing monstrous combat power all along! But Joshua is hanging on by sheer willpower. His juvenile chest is heaving, his vision is darkening, and every muscle fiber is screaming in agonizing mana backlash. He knows that if Rols smells even an ounce of weakness, the guard might attack. So our boy straightens his spine and locks eyes with him. For the first time in ten years, Joshua opens his mouth and speaks with the chilling authority of an emperor delivering a royal decree. He tells Rols that from this exact moment forward, anyone—including every centurion in the estate—who dares insult his mother will never be spared. Rols once survived the horrific slaughter at the Battle of Kraden Plains. And the aura pouring off this ten-year-old child feels identical to the mythical Nine Stars of the continent! The terrified guard completely loses his mind, shrieking that the kid is a literal demon, and sprints away as fast as his legs can carry him. Joshua stands in silence until the guard's footsteps completely fade into the distance, maintaining his intimidating battle posture until the coast is clear. But the second Rols is gone, the severe backlash of forcing foreign mana through an untrained ten-year-old vessel crashes down like a tidal wave. His legs give out completely, face-planting into the turf with a muffled curse as his consciousness fades into total blackness. Two armed centurions knocked cold, a survivor spreading rumors of an awakened prodigy, and the ducal palace about to explode into sheer chaos.
