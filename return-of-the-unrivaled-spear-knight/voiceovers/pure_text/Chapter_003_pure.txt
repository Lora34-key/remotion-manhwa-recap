In medieval high society, news travels fast—but scandalous castle rumors travel faster than lightning. By the time the morning bells ring across the Agnus Duchy—one of the five sovereign pillars holding up the Avalon Empire—the mansion is in absolute uproar. Down by the servant quarters, three kitchen workers—Paul, Brown, and Bob—are huddled together like gossiping teenagers trading top-secret intelligence. Paul drops the bombshell: that supposed mute orphan living out by the horse dung just pounded three seasoned imperial centurions into mashed rice cakes! Brown panics, hissing at Paul to watch his mouth. Because if the boy really has that kind of demonic combat skill, every servant who bullied him is in mortal danger. But Bob points out the most terrifying detail: Joshua is barely ten years old! That makes him four years younger than Master Babel, the family's crown prodigy. For perspective, the head of this family, Duke Aden von Agnus, is one of the legendary Nine Stars of the continent—a monster who swings a two-meter blade with one hand. Even the great Duke didn't awaken mana until age sixteen. So when his eldest son Babel unlocked mana at age fourteen, the empire declared him an immortal genius. Paul laughs it off, claiming the centurions must have faked the whole thing just to get closer to Joshua's gorgeous mother, Lucia. The cook starts bragging aloud about how he would love to get a taste of Lucia's body—completely unaware of the reaper walking up right behind him. Standing in the doorway with his back straight and shoulders squared like an imperial general is our boy Joshua, staring straight through their souls. Gone is the hunched, filthy boy who took beatings in silence. The crushing dignity rolling off him makes the three grown men choke on their own spit. In a voice completely devoid of childhood fear, Joshua gives them a single directive: go to the master's study and deliver a message. 'Tell the Duke that on his summons... I, Joshua von Agnus, have arrived.' He doesn't call himself a bastard—he claims the ducal name without flinching. Meanwhile, up in the Duke's inner sanctum, the atmosphere is heavy enough to snap iron. Flanked by Count Chiffon—the ruthless commander of the elite Red Knights—Duke Aden is staring down at Centurion Rols, who is trembling on his belly. Aden confirms Rols's rank in the 29th Battalion with a quiet, terrifying calm, before gesturing toward the evidence lying on the table. Resting on the desk is Rols's issued cuirass, crafted from the bulletproof hides of northern black orcs. But the breastplate is blown wide open like paper! Duke Aden runs his fingers along the torn leather, asking with dangerous stillness if a nine-year-old child really shattered black orc armor with his bare knuckles. Rols sobs that he isn't lying, but Count Chiffon refuses to buy a single word of it. Chiffon points out that even high-tier B-class knights need full-body mana infusion to tear orc hide. A nine-year-old awakening mana is completely unprecedented in empire history! The Red Knight commander concludes that Rols is spinning a fairy tale to cover up some shady criminal blunder, and offers to drag him outside for immediate execution. Rols weeps in terror, begging for mercy—until Duke Aden speaks three words that freeze everyone in their tracks: 'That is enough, Chiffon.' Duke Aden stands up, his towering two-meter frame casting a shadow that swallows the entire room. He decides he will inspect the boy with his own two eyes. Chiffon is stunned speechless. Even the legitimate heir Babel never gets personal visits from the Duke! Yet Aden is going down to see an illegitimate stable boy. The Duke strides out of his office with Chiffon trailing right behind, leaving a puddle of yellow shame underneath the sobbing centurion. Down in the grand corridor, our boy Joshua is standing motionless in front of an ornate oil painting of Emperor Marcus Van Britton. Looking at the imperial features, Joshua's mind immediately superimposes Kaiser's smug, treacherous face over the canvas. The sheer fury of being butchered like a dog in his past life bubbles up, and an oppressive heat haze begins leaking from his juvenile frame. But right in the middle of his emotional breakdown, someone decides to interrupt. Enter fourteen-year-old Babel von Agnus, dressed in fine silk. Babel scoffs in disgust, demanding to know what business a manure-shoveling bastard has inside the main palace corridor. Joshua doesn't even blink. He treats the family's prized heir like empty space, which sends Babel's fragile teenage ego into absolute orbit. Screaming that a peasant will pay for ignoring him, Babel winds up and throws an arrogant sucker punch straight at the back of Joshua's head! Big mistake. Joshua doesn't even flinch. He reaches behind his shoulder and snatches Babel's incoming punch right out of the air like catching a fly! And then our boy slowly tightens his fingers. A horrific series of bone-crunching pops echoes down the vaulted hallway: crack, crack, crack! Babel drops to his knees in sheer agony. His prized knuckles are being crushed into dust, and the realization hits him: the stable rumors were one hundred percent real! Humiliated beyond belief, Babel ignites mana into his free fist, preparing to incinerate his little brother where he stands. But before mana can erupt, a thunderous voice shakes the foundations of the palace: 'WHAT DO YOU THINK YOU ARE DOING?!' The Duke has arrived. Babel immediately snuffs out his flame, drops both knees to the marble floor, and stammers that they were merely sharing fraternal greetings. Aden doesn't even look at his kneeling heir. His piercing gaze locks straight onto Joshua, waiting to see how the illegitimate son will greet his sovereign lord. And our boy Joshua? He doesn't kneel. He doesn't prostrate. He gives Duke Aden a casual, respectful nod—like two guild masters crossing paths on the road! Count Chiffon loses his absolute mind, screaming if the kid is insane for not bowing before one of the five continental Masters. But Duke Aden is fascinated. He raises a hand to quiet Chiffon, taking slow, deliberate steps toward Joshua until their boots are inches apart. Joshua forces his racing pulse to slow down. Aden is a Master whose sensory acuity can detect an errant heartbeat from fifty paces. The Duke asks the million-dollar question: 'Is it true that you manipulated mana?' From the floor, Babel grits his teeth until blood seeps from his gums. To Babel, the thought of his peasant half-brother having magic is an unforgivable blasphemy. But Aden isn't interested in words—he wants physical proof. Faster than human eyes can track, Duke Aden's white-gloved hand clamps around Joshua's slender wrist like a vice! A concentrated river of pure Master-level mana floods into Joshua's arm, scanning every meridian, blood vessel, and spiritual node in his body. At first, Aden expects to find an empty vessel. But as his energy pushes deeper toward the boy's lower abdomen, the Duke's stone-cold face completely shatters. The Duke gasps aloud, his grip trembling. In his entire four decades of warfare, he has never encountered an internal anatomy like this! Resting right where the mana hall should be is a monstrous, ancient blockage—a temporal seal left behind by Lugia's dimensional regression! To Count Chiffon and Babel, seeing the continent's greatest warlord look terrified is more shocking than an enemy army invading the castle. Aden stammers in disbelief: 'This is...!' He knows that according to standard imperial medicine, this boy should be a crippled invalid who can't even stand. Yet this supposed invalid just shattered orc hide armor with his bare knuckles, forcing the greatest Master in the empire to question everything he knows.
