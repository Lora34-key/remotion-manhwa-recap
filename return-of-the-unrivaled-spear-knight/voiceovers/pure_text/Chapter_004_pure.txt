Right in the middle of the grand palace corridor, Duke Aden von Agnus has his iron grip clamped around our boy's wrist, scanning his internal mana channels. But instead of finding the roaring aura of a martial genius, the Duke's stoic warlord expression completely shatters into disbelief. The Duke mutters in utter astonishment: 'There is nothing... not a single drop of mana inside this child.' From the floor, Babel instantly smirks like a spoiled brat who just got handed a win, while Count Chiffon chuckles that the rumors were pure nonsense. But Duke Aden cuts them off immediately. He isn't talking about a missing mana hall; in this world, every living creature possesses baseline biological mana! Commoners have it, woodland rabbits have it, even magical beasts have it. A total zero-mana biological signature only belongs to one thing: a walking undead corpse. And right where Joshua's lower abdomen should circulate energy, the Duke detects something foul, sticky, and revolting—a bizarre temporal blockage. Count Chiffon brings up the dreaded 'Congenital Mana Disorder'—a rare one-in-a-hundred-thousand curse where children are born unable to store mana, guaranteed to die before eighteen. But Duke Aden is no ordinary knight. He knows a true cripple could never fracture northern black orc skin with bare hands. Something far deeper is happening here. So the Duke turns on his heel and drops a bombshell: 'Prepare a private guest room in the main mansion. Joshua will stay here from now on.' Babel loses his composure, shouting that an illegitimate servant's son has no right to live in the ducal mansion! But Aden's icy glare shuts him up instantly. 'Is there an issue, Babel?' Aden asks with razor-sharp quietness. Babel swallows his golden pride, lowers his head, and mumbles that there is no problem at all. Next, the Duke commands Count Chiffon to oversee Joshua's basic training starting tomorrow. And if the boy shows aptitude, to teach him the Red Knights' secret arts! Before departing, Aden locks eyes with Joshua one last time, murmuring that he looks forward to seeing what becomes of him when he returns from the capital in one year. Count Chiffon escorts Joshua down the executive wing to a private VIP room that is larger and more lavish than an entire peasant family estate. Chiffon is visibly annoyed that this former stable boy doesn't even show a shred of gratitude for living like royalty, before slamming the door shut. Left alone, our boy lets his small frame sink deep into the plush feather mattress, exhaling a heavy breath as the setting sun paints the room crimson. He replays Duke Aden's parting words. The Duke spends nearly all his time in the imperial capital of Arcadia serving the crown. Why would he set a one-year deadline? In his previous life, having his secrets known was the leash they used to enslave him. If he wants absolute sovereignty, he needs his grandmaster strength back—fast. He avoided visiting his mother during the day to prevent Duchess Vanessa's spies from targeting her. But once the castle clock strikes midnight, our boy makes his move. He vaults over the marble railing, dropping three stories into the moonlit gardens with the silent grace of a ghost stepping on silk. Touching down in the shadows, he tests his internal energy flow. The muscular soreness from earlier has subsided, but the second he pulls mana inward... ...the repulsive knot in his core detonates like a powder keg! Joshua drops to one knee, clutching his stomach as two opposing divine and demonic forces collide! Right in the center of the spiritual storm, the phantom silhouette of his demonic spear Lugia flares to life, actively suppressing both warring energies! Lugia didn't just reverse his age—it bound divine and demonic powers together in his soul! If Joshua learns to control all three, he will transcend mortal knight limits entirely. Hearing patrolling sentries approach, Joshua slips past the torchlight like a shadow, sprinting straight toward his old childhood barn to see his mother. But just as the weathered wooden cabin comes into sight, a blood-curdling scream rips through the midnight silence: 'KYAAAA! Let go of me!' It is his mother Lucia! Every ounce of rational restraint burns away in a fraction of a microsecond as our boy bolts toward the doorway. Inside the dimly lit barn, centurion Roid is forcibly pinning weeping Lucia against the wooden floorboards, while Gort steps forward unsheathing a hunting knife! Gort spits venom, screaming that because her demonic brat humiliated them, their military standing was ruined, and he is going to make her pay in blood! Gort rips Lucia's collar, while Rols frantically begs him to stop, terrified that the ten-year-old monster from this morning might walk through that door. Gort shoves Rols to the ground, roaring that Duchess Vanessa herself promised them total immunity. If Rols is terrified of a toddler, he can get lost! Gort raises the knife to Lucia's throat, grinning like a rabid animal—until a freezing chill drops the barn temperature below freezing. A single word echoes across the barn with the crushing resonance of an executioner's bell: 'STOP.' Standing in the moonlit doorway is Joshua, shrouded in an overwhelming crimson aura so thick you could carve it with a broadsword. In a voice drenched in absolute slaughter, he delivers a verdict: 'Don't even dream of walking out of this room alive.' Rols practically weeps in terror, screaming that the demon has returned. But Gort stubbornly tries to laugh it off, barking that Joshua is just an unarmed kid. Joshua takes three slow, deliberate steps forward, and utters two chilling words: 'Get lost.' Roid sneers, thinking the boy is talking to them. But Joshua wasn't looking at the centurions—his eyes are locked directly onto the ceiling rafters! 'Oh? How on earth did you manage to sense me?' a suave voice chuckles from above as a cloaked figure drops lightly to the floorboards. Standing before them is a devastatingly handsome warrior with sharp amber eyes and a rapier at his hip. The centurions choke on their breath: 'SIR CAIN?!' Glinting on his chest is the legendary Golden Red Cross—marking him as one of only three supreme champions appointed directly by Duke Aden himself! Joshua deduces that Count Chiffon sent this elite stalker to watch him. The three guards quickly scramble into clumsy salutes, praying Cain won't intervene. Cain studies Joshua with fascinated amusement, wondering how a ten-year-old detected his master-tier stealth when even veteran knights couldn't. But our boy doesn't act like a frightened child begging for rescue. He steps toward Cain and poses a calculated legal question. 'These soldiers attempted to assault a member under the Duke's direct protection. According to the Duke's martial penal code... how do we handle this?' As Joshua speaks, he unleashes the soul pressure of an unvanquished Grandmaster who conquered empires in his previous life! Sir Cain's breath hitches. His knees physically buckle under the tyrannical gravity of this ten-year-old child's sheer presence! The playful smirk vanishes from Cain's face. He realizes he isn't speaking to a noble concubine's bastard—he is answering an absolute monarch. Cain draws his sword an inch from its scabbard, delivering the law with chilling finality: 'Under the Duke's law... it would be IMMEDIATE EXECUTION.' The centurions freeze in paralyzing terror as they realize Duchess Vanessa's promises of immunity mean nothing in front of Duke Aden's personal executioner. Sir Cain drops into a lethal low-stance lunge, scarlet Red Knight aura igniting along his steel blade like a butcher's fire. While the crimson execution begins behind him, Joshua kneels beside his trembling mother, wrapping his arm around her shoulders to shield her eyes. The past life of helplessness is over. The bullies will bleed, the elite Red Knight will be bent into Joshua's first loyal vassal, and the real war has only just begun.
