If there is one rule in the Agnus estate you never break, it's this: when an elite Red Knight unsheathes steel, bodies are about to hit the floor. With a single fluid step that barely disturbs the air, Sir Cain's rapier flashes in an arc of scarlet mana, cleaving Gort open before he can blink. Roid tries to swing his broadsword in wild panic, but Cain parries the blade with a deafening metallic clang that rattles Roid's teeth. Cain's counter-thrust pierces clean through Roid's chest, leaving the second centurion gurgling apologies into a pool of his own crimson blood. In under five seconds, two armed thugs are erased from existence. Cain turns his icy amber eyes toward the last surviving coward: Rols. Rols sobs and claws at the floorboards as Cain raises his sword for the decapitation blow—until a calm voice cuts through the barn: 'STOP.' Cain's blade halts a hair's breadth from Rols's throat. The Red Knight frowns, reminding Joshua that insulting ducal kin requires total execution. But our boy Joshua is already analyzing Cain's fencing form. Young, disciplined, and clearly the strongest C-rank talent in the Red Knight order. Instead of thanking him for the rescue, Joshua looks Cain dead in the eyes and asks two simple questions that shift the entire balance of power. 'Who is your master?' Joshua asks. Cain puffs his chest with military pride, answering that he serves only Duke Aden von Agnus. Then our boy points a thumb at his own chest and drops the hammer: 'Then who am I?' The room goes dead silent. The unspoken subtext hits Cain like a physical blow: *I carry Duke Aden's blood. If you serve the Duke, you obey me.* 'Sheathe your sword,' Joshua commands. Cain hesitates for a heartbeat, then slowly slides his blade home with a crisp metallic click. Seeing his life spared, Rols frantically bangs his head against the wood, cutting his own forehead while swearing eternal loyalty to Joshua. But our boy scoffs in cold disgust. He didn't spare Rols out of mercy, and he certainly doesn't want the loyalty of an unprincipled turncoat. Joshua gives him a single job: run back to the barracks and tell every single soldier in the 40,000-man army exactly what happened here tonight. And if anyone else in this estate dares bare their fangs at his mother again, Joshua won't summon a knight—he will tear their pathetic lives apart himself! Rols scrambles out of the barn on all fours, shrieking in pure terror and sprinting into the darkness like the devil himself is biting his heels. Once the coward is gone, Sir Cain crosses his arms and chuckles, asking if this poised warlord is really the manure-shoveling mute from the rumors. Joshua laughs it off: 'The Duke's mistake? The common maid's bastard? Believe whatever rumors help you sleep at night.' But Cain doesn't laugh. He explains the ancient imperial code: at age twenty-three, every knight of the realm has the sacred right to choose which master they will serve for life. Cain reveals that his choice arrives next year. And while common sense says to back the legitimate heir Babel, Cain trusts his instincts: in one year, he will pledge his sword to Joshua! Before Joshua can even reply, Cain vanishes into the midnight air. Our boy shakes his head with a smirk: 'What an absolute oddball.' A soft, fragile voice pulls him back to reality: 'J-Joshua?!' Lucia slowly opens her emerald eyes, sitting up from the straw. She throws her arms around him, weeping that her baby is safe. And right there, the cold, ruthless Grandmaster melts into a sobbing child in his mother's embrace. In his previous timeline, she died cold and alone from illness when he was fourteen. But this time, he thanks the heavens for a second chance—he will protect her with his life. Stepping out under the moonlight, our boy reaffirms his true identity: he is no longer the timid stable boy—he is Joshua Sanders, the Strongest Spear Knight in human history! Meanwhile, back at the main ducal palace, high-stakes political maneuvering is already brewing. Count Chiffon informs Duke Aden that an influential guest has arrived unannounced: Count Ponselle Jin Rebreka, head of an imperial magic powerhouse. Even more surprising, the Count didn't come alone—he brought his prized daughter, the young Lady Isellin Jin Rebreka. While Count Rebreka holds lower noble rank than a Great Duke, his family's magical research and political clout across Avalon cannot be ignored. And his nine-year-old daughter, Isellin, is the talk of the entire empire. She is an unprecedented magic prodigy who reached 2nd-Class magic by age nine! If congenital mana disorder is the continent's worst curse, then Isellin received the divine opposite: the legendary 'Mana Blessing,' allowing her to commune with nature effortlessly. Chiffon assumes Rebreka is here to pitch a political marriage for Master Babel, who turns fifteen next year and needs an illustrious bride for his coming-of-age ceremony. But Duke Aden immediately shakes his head. Babel is already informally promised to Lady Cheryl of the Pontier family—a house with bottomless mercantile wealth. The Duke stops dead in his tracks. Count Chiffon turns back, wondering why his lord is staring off into space. Then Duke Aden drops a totally unexpected question: 'How is Joshua doing?' Chiffon stammers in confusion. Why on earth is the Duke asking about the concubine's cripple when imperial nobility is waiting in the parlor? Duke Aden flashes a wicked smirk and drops the biggest bombshell in ducal history: 'I'm thinking of betrothing Lady Isellin to Joshua.' Count Chiffon's brain practically short-circuits. He screams aloud, asking if his lord has completely lost his mind! 'You want to marry Lady Isellin—who received the supreme Mana Blessing—to a stable boy suffering from the terminal Mana Curse?!' Aden doesn't explain his logic. A continental Master judges value through raw combat results, not superstitious gossip. Little does our boy know he's being drafted into a political power play, because right now, Joshua is focused on an ancient mystery. Thousands of years ago, before the continent split into East and West, the mighty Aslan Empire ruled the world from its capital, Iris—which lies buried beneath the Agnus estate! Only Joshua knows this secret. North of the stables, right behind a house-sized boulder, lies a hidden entrance down into the forgotten abyss. He slips into the fissure before dawn breaks, descending ancient stone stairs worn smooth by forgotten millennia. As the air grows heavy with primordial mana, Joshua whispers a single name: 'Lugia.' The very weapon that made him the continent's apex predator. The narrow tunnel suddenly opens up into a jaw-dropping cavern the size of a mountain valley, illuminated by pulsating ancient runes. Sprawling before his eyes are the colossal ruins of Iris—majestic white marble bridges spanning bottomless chasms and monolithic cathedrals untouched by time. Standing like a tiny speck in the colossal subterranean metropolis, our boy walks forward with zero hesitation. At the end of the sacred plaza stands a soaring altar. And inside that altar rests the legendary red spear that chose him as its master. The closer he steps, the more his internal energy lump stabilizes—humming in perfect harmony with the dormant relic waiting above. In his previous life, finding this took him decades of hardship. But today, at age ten, the prodigy claims his god-slaying weapon on day one. The spear that conquered an empire is about to be unsealed forty years early—and the entire continent's destiny is about to shatter.
