High in the northern garden of the Agnus estate sits a massive five-meter boulder known to guards as the sacred 'Guardian Stone.' In his previous life, after his mother died, young Joshua would climb this forbidden rock every night just to talk to the stars and pretend she was listening. Running his fingers across the top, he locates a microscopic needle-thin crevice—the secret trigger to the forgotten subterranean capital of Iris. Without hesitating, our boy bites his right thumb, letting drops of fresh crimson blood drip down into the ancient mechanism. The rock shudders as concentric ancient runes flare with blinding cyan light, tearing open a spatial rift right in front of him! Joshua dives straight into the swirling vortex, letting the spatial currents carry his small body down into the forgotten abyss beneath the castle. He touches down inside the cavernous temple of Iris, but immediately pauses. The grand hall is cracked, weathered, and covered in millennia of dust. In his previous life, he found this place at age fifteen, where his demonic spear Lugia stood bound in sacred silver chains atop the central altar. But reaching the pedestal, his jaw drops in utter disbelief. There is no demonic spear. Resting on the cold stone is a rusty, plain iron rod! Our boy stares in total confusion: did someone rebuild the temple over the next six years, or is this battered metal stick really his weapon? The exact millisecond his skin touches the iron, an explosion of blinding white radiance detonates, pulling his soul into another dimension! Joshua blinks his eyes open to find himself standing in an infinite, pure white mental space where neither shadows nor sound exist. 'Over here,' a deep, timeless voice echoes from behind. Joshua spins around, bracing for battle as a pillar of dark smoke coalesces in the void. Floating inside the dark mist is the iron rod. But before Joshua can call it a stick, the weapon speaks with thunderous majesty. 'I am not an iron rod,' the weapon rumbles. 'I am the Demon God's Spirit, Lugia!' The realization hits our boy: when Lugia used its life essence to rewind forty years of continental time, its godly form degraded into this primordial state! Lugia warns him that time is rapidly expiring. The two opposing divine and demonic energies inside his ten-year-old body are slowly devouring his lifespan. There is only one way to harmonize the warring powers and unseal his dormant god-tier strength: Joshua must reach Magic Spear Arts Stage 4 in record time! To understand why Stage 4 is ridiculous, look at how martial rankings work in the Avalon Empire. Nobles treat swordsmanship as the only sacred path, mocking spears as flimsy sticks meant for disposable commoner conscripts. A warrior who can materialize pure mana into an aura blade is ranked as an A-class Master—an 'Absolute Being' like Duke Aden. Across the continent, only five exist. In his past life, Joshua achieved Stage 5—the pinnacle of the Magic Spear Arts—becoming the undisputed strongest human being alive with a weapon nobles laughed at. Stage 4 is the equivalent of an imperial Master. While our boy already knows the techniques by heart, cultivating without an active mana hall should be impossible. But Lugia smirks: 'Do not worry. As long as you wield me, I will regulate your core, allowing you to channel mana with zero physical recoil!' With a brilliant flash, Joshua's consciousness snaps back into reality. His hands are firmly clamped around the iron rod, and the connection is electric! The agonizing knot in his abdomen instantly subsides. Atmospheric mana pours through his arms like a rushing waterfall, completely regulated by Lugia's soul. He drops into a low combat stance, spinning the heavy iron rod around his back with the effortless fluidity of an immortal spear divinity. He executes the opening foundation of the ancient art: Magic Spear Arts Stage 1! He condenses his entire mana reserve into a single microscopic point at the rod's tip. And thrusts! An explosive azure shockwave detonates with the force of an artillery shell, tearing a supersonic sonic boom clean across the subterranean temple! Dust and shattered stone rain down as our boy exhales a vapor breath, grinning from ear to ear. Stage 1 is mastered on his very first attempt! 'This is where it truly begins,' Joshua whispers into the darkness. 'Kaiser Van Britton... I will grow stronger, reclaim my throne, and tear your empire to ash.' Meanwhile, up in the lavish ducal reception hall, the atmosphere is dripping with aristocratic tension. Duke Aden and Count Chiffon enter with military precision, but Aden immediately halts. The reception hall holds an unexpected crowd. Standing near the window is Princess Cheryl of the Pontier family—a fiery red-haired prodigy whose family controls the empire's largest merchant fleet. Beside her, Count Lok Roman Pontier—the financial brain of the Pontier clan—frantically bows, apologizing that Princess Cheryl was too impatient to wait for an invitation. Duke Aden laughs it off with characteristic swagger, casually joking that if they feel that guilty, they should just pledge their entire fortune to the Agnus family! Cheryl playfully scoffs that the Duke is as greedy as ever, trying to rob another noble house of its crown jewel in broad daylight. She cuts straight to business, asking if it's true that young Babel reached C-rank knight status at age fourteen, hinting that she wants to recruit him as her future consort. Roman panics over the girl's bluntness, but Aden waves it off, enjoying her sharp wit—before turning his gaze across the opposite side of the hall. His expression shifts from grandfatherly amusement to cold political calculation as his eyes land on the second delegation waiting in the wings. 'My apologies, Ponselle,' Aden says with smooth mockery. 'I was so distracted by the Pontier family, I nearly forgot you were standing there.' Standing beside the Count is young Lady Isellin, dressed in an immaculate navy gown with turquoise hair that seems to pulse with natural ambient mana. Even at nine years old, her mana control is breathtaking. Duke Aden can feel the atmospheric spirits bowing to her presence like a little magic queen. Count Rebreka came here with one singular objective: to link his magical dynasty to Duke Agnus's military juggernaut through a marriage alliance. Aden looks at Princess Cheryl, then looks at Lady Isellin. The pieces on the continental chessboard are falling into place faster than anyone anticipated. If Babel is promised to the gold of House Pontier, then pairing Lady Isellin's god-given Mana Blessing with Joshua's mysterious power creates an unstoppable faction. Deep beneath the palace foundations, oblivious to the political storm brewing above his head, our boy Joshua rests his iron rod against his palm. He runs his thumb across the iron rod. One thin layer of rust has already flaked off, revealing the faint demonic crimson sheen of Lugia's true body! Every stage of the Magic Spear Arts he unlocks will restore a portion of Lugia's god-tier form until the legendary red weapon is fully reborn. The first rays of morning sun filter down through the ceiling fissures. Time is up—knight training with Count Chiffon begins at daybreak. He climbs the ancient stairs, returning to the surface world not as a broken concubine's bastard, but as an awakened dragon sharpening its claws. Across the estate, the Red Knights are gathering for morning drills, unaware that their newest recruit is about to dismantle their entire worldview. Babel is nursing his bruised knuckles, waiting for a chance to humiliate his little brother during formal combat training. An illegitimate son carrying a rusty rod, a furious heir thirsty for revenge, and the first official spar that will set the entire empire on fire.
