Vanessa shrieks in pure hysteria: how on earth could a maid's child wield mana better than her prized heir?! Chiffon wipes blood from his mouth and drops the real nightmare: Duke Aden has decided to bring Joshua along to the imperial capital, Arcadia! In the Avalon Empire, registering as an official C-rank knight requires dueling an imperial royal knight in the capital under the emperor's gaze. Aden lets out a rare sigh, observing that his youngest has changed completely since their last encounter, asking softly: 'Do you resent me?' Joshua calmly replies that he holds no resentment. This isn't just a kid who knows how to swing a stick—his strategic intellect is downright terrifying! 'Then you already know the solution,' Aden remarks. Joshua nods: 'Take me to the capital and enroll me in the Imperial Academy.' Aden agrees, offering something unprecedented: whenever he is in the capital, he will personally tutor Joshua in master-level swordsmanship! An imperial ambush, a four-meter mutated monster, and a nine-year-old reaper about to test Magic Spear Stage 1 on live prey. The slaughter begins now!
