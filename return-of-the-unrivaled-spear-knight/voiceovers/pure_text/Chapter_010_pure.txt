Right after stepping out of Duke Aden's private office, our boy Joshua can still feel his fingertips tingling from the sheer pressure of facing an imperial Master. Even after regressing with decades of combat memory, standing before one of the Nine Stars in a nine-year-old body is enough to get anyone's blood pumping. But before Joshua can take three steps down the carpeted corridor, an obnoxious voice calls out from the shadows: Babel has been waiting for him. Joshua doesn't even break stride. He hits him with the ultimate disrespect, saying he has zero interest in wasting breath on a sore loser. Babel loses his mind, shouting for an immediate rematch. He claims their first fight was just child's play with wooden toys, and this time he wants real steel. Joshua notices something unexpected in Babel's glare. The petty arrogance is gone, replaced by a desperate, burning hunger to prove his martial worth. That genuine spark of warrior pride earns a slight smirk from our boy. He asks if Babel truly has the confidence to step onto the execution block again. Meanwhile, hundreds of leagues away in the heart of the continent, the camera pans over Galtika Palace, the towering seat of the Avalon Empire. Inside the cavernous courtroom, heavy silence reigns over the crimson carpets, broken only by the cold draft whistling through the high stained glass. On the throne sits Emperor Marcus von Britton—the Iron-Blooded Monarch whose overwhelming presence radiates the lethality of a true continental Master. Prostrated before him is the commander of the Black Wind—the sovereign's personal shadow network that watches every heartbeat across the empire. The spy delivers an urgent report: a monstrous new talent with the potential to become a continental star has suddenly emerged within the Agnus Duchy. Marcus assumes his sister Vanessa gave birth to another royal prodigy, but the spy clarifies that this boy has zero imperial blood in his veins. He was born to a common maid from the servant quarters—a despised bastard whose very existence was hidden away in the horse stables. Instead of anger, Emperor Marcus roars with laughter. Having Babel was already envious enough, but Duke Agnus has somehow pulled a second miracle out of thin air. The spy reminds him that his own sons possess unmatched royal blood, but Marcus dismisses them. Politically gifted they may be, but martially, they are mediocre at best. Seeing the Emperor's good mood, the spy drops the real bomb: verified reports confirm that Babel, the empire's golden child, was completely demolished by this boy. Marcus freezes on his throne. Babel is a recognized C-rank knight at fourteen. For an older teen to beat him is possible—so Marcus asks how old this bastard is. When the spy replies that Joshua only just turned nine, the temperature in the imperial throne room drops below freezing. Marcus stands tall, trembling with adrenaline. To beat a mana user, you must wield mana yourself—meaning a nine-year-old child is already operating at C-rank! The Duke has formally requested the Imperial Knight Order to conduct a certification exam. Marcus wants to ride out himself, but geopolitical tensions forbid it. Instead, Marcus issues an imperial mandate: dispatch one of his royal sons alongside the knights. An emperor must possess an eye for talent, and this is their test. The scene cuts back to the secluded training yard behind the ducal estate, where Babel has brought Joshua for their secret, unauthorized duel. Babel draws a razor-sharp steel longsword, completely discarding the rules. Thick mana wraps around the blade like a hungry viper! Babel arrogantly offers him a spare sword, but our boy Joshua raises his bare hands. For someone of Babel's caliber, flesh and bone are more than enough. Enraged beyond reason, Babel lunges with full C-rank velocity, aiming a lethal strike straight at Joshua's unprotected throat! With millimetric precision, Joshua slips under the steel edge like smoke, causing Babel's sword to whistle uselessly through empty air. A brutal barehanded punch smashes directly into the flat of the sword! The violent vibration tears Babel's grip and sends shockwaves up his arm. Joshua lectures him mid-clash: knights look down on hand-to-hand combat as commoner brawling, but real martial arts turn the entire human body into a lethal weapon. Concentrated sapphire mana suddenly coats Joshua's bare knuckles! Babel is horrified—infusing raw mana directly into flesh without a weapon is Master-tier control! Right before impact, Joshua detonates the compressed mana at point-blank range, unleashing his exclusive signature technique: Aura Explosion! The resulting shockwave obliterates the ground! The concentrated kinetic blast bends Babel's mastercrafted steel sword like a cheap tin fork! Babel is hurled across the arena, smashing into the stone wall and spitting a mouthful of blood as his weapon clatters uselessly to the flagstones. Lying in the dirt, Babel's reality collapses. He finally realizes how small he is—this illegitimate boy isn't just talented, he is an untouchable monster. Joshua towers over him with icy disdain. He echoes the Duke's exact words: if this humiliation eats away at your soul, then stop whining and get stronger. A terrifying wave of murderous aura washes over Babel. Joshua reminds him that for all the cruelty inflicted on his mother, he could easily butcher him right here. Because Babel showed genuine martial determination, Joshua spares his limbs—with one final warning: dare cross that line again, and he will permanently sever his sword hand! As our boy walks away, Babel clutches his bent sword in total defeat, realizing the chasm between them is too wide to ever cross. The next morning in his new high tower chambers, Joshua wakes up feeling completely drained after consecutively deploying high-level mana in an unconditioned body. Absorbing Lugia stabilized his spiritual core, but a nine-year-old vessel still suffers physical fatigue when channeling adult-tier mana bursts. A polite knock rattles the chamber door. Assuming Sir Cain has returned with his supply inventory, Joshua pulls on his vest and walks over to unlock it. He swings the door open saying he didn't expect Cain so early—only to freeze when he's met with a radiant girl dressed in an elegant lavender gown! It is none other than Princess Cheryl de Pontier, the sole heiress of the northern Pontier Duchy, smiling brightly like an older sister paying a friendly visit! Cheryl playfully suggests he can call her Noona since she's three years his senior, stepping right past him to invite herself into his room for tea! But our boy Joshua does not give a single care about high noble charm. He plants his hand flat on her shoulder and shoves her straight back into the hallway! He looks her straight in the eyes, tells her to get lost, and slams the heavy door right in the aristocratic princess's face with a thunderous BANG! Cheryl is left frozen in the hallway, completely flabbergasted. No noble in the entire empire has ever dared treat the Pontier jewel like an unwanted solicitor! Inside, Joshua shakes his head at how exhausting politicians are. But barely two seconds later, another insistent series of knocks rattles the door frame! Our boy snaps. He thinks this spoiled princess clearly cannot comprehend English and needs a physical demonstration to understand the concept of personal space! He rips the door open and roars at the top of his lungs to get lost—only to realize he's screaming directly into the stunned face of Sir Cain! Cain stammers in terror, wondering if he interrupted something scandalous. Joshua quickly clears his throat, awkwardly apologizing for the friendly fire. Shifting back to business, Joshua asks if the secret preparations are complete. Cain nods, reporting that every carriage, steed, and crate is locked and loaded. But Cain can't help asking the burning question on his mind: why is a nine-year-old boy plotting clandestine logistical movements like a seasoned grand strategist? Joshua turns toward the horizon with a confident grin. He tells Cain that blind trust is all he needs—because when the empire's wolves finally bare their fangs, everything will make terrifying sense!
