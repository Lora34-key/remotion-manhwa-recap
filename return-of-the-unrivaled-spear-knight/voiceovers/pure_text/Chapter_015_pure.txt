With the mana evaluation officially concluded, the imperial knight battalion packs their banners and begins their march back to Arcadia. The ducal courtyard is buzzing with hectic activity as stable hands load heavy baggage carts for Duke Aden's sudden northern campaign. Walking calmly through the chaos is our boy Joshua, wearing an elegant travel cloak and admiring a brilliant silver ring on his finger: the Don Ring. A flashback reveals Duke Aden summoning Joshua for a private briefing, sliding an ornate velvet box across his desk as an unexpected gift. Aden reveals that decades ago, he assisted the Master of the Magic Tower, who gifted him this sacred artifact crafted by his own hands. The Don Ring holds spatial enchantment: with a single injection of mana, full-body enchanted plate armor materializes over the wearer in milliseconds! Joshua teases his father, pointing out that an artifact of this caliber was strictly reserved for the designated heir of the duchy. Aden awkwardly clears his throat, barking that it was merely gathering dust in the armory and Joshua is only borrowing it for protection. Aden explains the real strategy: to navigate the treacherous vipers of Arcadia, Joshua will enter the capital under an alias, posing as a nameless viscount's heir. The armor carries hidden ducal runes—a silent warning to imperial royals that behind this mysterious child stands the wrath of Duke Aden himself! Then Duke Aden hesitates. In a rare moment of vulnerability, the iron-blooded warrior asks quietly: 'Joshua... do you hold resentment toward me?' Joshua falls silent. In his past life, Duke Aden neglected him and his mother, leading directly to Lucia's agonizing death in the servant quarters. But Joshua also remembers the brutal tragedy ten years later: Duke Aden falling in the imperial civil war, pierced by dozens of treacherous arrows in the mud. Time washes away old bitterness. In this new life, a bond that never existed before has begun to take root between father and son. Back in the present courtyard, a smooth, honeyed voice interrupts his thoughts: Prince Kaiser von Britton is walking over with a radiant smile. Flanked by Valmont and Evergrant, Kaiser playfully asks whether he should address him as Sir Joshua now that he holds royal knight status. Our boy hits him with an unenthusiastic deadpan glare, asking what business the fourth prince could possibly have with him. Kaiser extends an official invitation, urging Joshua to visit his palace the moment he arrives in the capital after clearing the northern monsters. Kaiser dangles massive bait: royal sponsorship can bypass the brutal annual entrance exams and exempt Joshua from ten years of mandatory military servitude! He frames the corrupt offer as innocent admiration, claiming that his political greed for Joshua's talent is simply too great to resist. Joshua feels sick to his stomach. That exact fake smile and honeyed tongue lured him into twenty years of thankless bloodshed in his previous life. If the imperial archmage weren't watching, Joshua would have summoned Lugia and driven thirty inches of divine steel straight through Kaiser's lying teeth! But Joshua needs imperial clearance for a greater purpose: deep inside the secret vaults of Galtika Palace sleeps his most cherished companion from his past life! Joshua waves his hand dismissively, casually telling the prince he'll think about it before turning on his heel and walking away! Valmont fumes at the boy's breathtaking disrespect, but Kaiser stops him with a dark, confident smirk: Joshua will fall into his net eventually. Joshua smirks back into the sun. The game has begun, and this time, the prince will be the one dancing like a puppet on strings! The expedition sets forth! Duke Aden leads an elite strike force of one hundred seasoned knights, thundering north toward the treacherous Loch Fief. Riding alongside the Duke are Count Chiffon and Master Magician Roman—the legendary Fourth Circle archmage who commands the dukedom's arcane corps. Beside Joshua rides Babel, keeping his eyes locked onto his brother the entire journey, burning with silent determination to prove his worth. Our boy laughs internally at Babel's intense staring contest before the lead scout halts the column outside the gates of Loch Castle! Loch Fief is a desolate frontier territory, bordering the abyssal wilderness known as the Forest of Dark Beings. Greeting them at the portcullis is Roben, a grizzled veteran of the Cloven Knights who dedicated decades of blood and sweat to the Agnus bloodline. Duke Aden dismounts and warmly greets the old veteran, expressing genuine remorse for having an honorable loyalist stationed in this desolate outpost. Roben places a hand over his breastplate, replying that being allowed to serve House Agnus even in retirement is his greatest lifelong privilege. Suddenly, heavy waddling footsteps shake the flagstones as a morbidly obese noble dressed in garish velvet bursts through the castle doors! This is Viscount Beeg, Lord of Loch Fief and a close maternal relative of Duchess Vanessa, sweating profusely with his young daughter Anna in tow. Duke Aden rubs his temples in immediate regret, thinking he should have bypassed the castle entirely and ridden straight into the monster woods. Beeg instantly turns on old knight Roben, loudly screeching that the veteran has gone senile for failing to announce the Duke's arrival with fanfares! As Roben endures the insults, Duke Aden steps in and roasts Beeg, remarking that the viscount seems to grow rounder and fatter every passing month. Beeg shamelessly giggles, rubbing his plump palms together and claiming that his expanding waistline is proof of the Duke's generous governance. In the background, Joshua and Babel share a rare moment of mutual agreement: this corrupt viscount is either completely brainless or impossibly shameless! Beeg turns to Babel with theatrical groveling, showering his high-born nephew in sickening praise about his burgeoning swordsmanship spirit. Babel brushes him off with a cool greeting. Beeg's eyes then wander over to Joshua, wondering who this unfamiliar child in fine traveling clothes could be. Beeg beams, assuming this is another high-ranking noble ward. Count Chiffon steps forward, awkwardly announcing that this is Lord Joshua. 'The second son of Duke Agnus,' Chiffon announces. Beeg blinks dumbly—when on earth did his cousin Vanessa conceive another child?! Chiffon pulls Beeg aside, frantically whispering the scandalous truth: Joshua has zero imperial royal blood—he was born to a common maid from the servant quarters! The jovial smile on Beeg's face instantly turns to stone. The warmth completely vanishes from his piggish eyes. Beeg nervously asks if the mother at least belonged to a fallen aristocratic family. Chiffon confirms she was nothing more than an illiterate commoner. Beeg turns back to face Joshua. The boy stands in front of him with tranquil, unwavering dignity, completely unbothered by the whispers. But in Beeg's poisoned mind, blood is everything. The sight of a commoner's bastard wearing fine ducal silks fills him with sickening revulsion. Looking down at the continent's youngest knight, the corrupt viscount sneers internally: 'Ugh... what a filthy, disgusting pig!' Joshua reads the fat noble's sneer instantly. Our boy has put hundreds of haughty aristocrats in early graves, and this corrupt pig is making the list! With treacherous nobles behind him and monstrous Black Ogres ahead in the dark woods, the stage is set for a brutal baptism of fire! An arrogant corrupt noble, a forest teeming with apex predators, and an unrivaled spear knight about to demonstrate who the real monster is. The bloodbath begins now!
