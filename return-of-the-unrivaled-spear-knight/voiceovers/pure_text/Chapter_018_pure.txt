Leaning heavily against his spear, our boy Joshua gasps for breath as acute fatigue washes over his prepubescent muscles. Gritting his teeth, Joshua sprints across the rocky ridgeline, desperate to warn the knights before slaughter strikes! Down at the encampment, pitch-black night settles over the valley under the blood-red moon, making sentries grip their spears with sweaty palms. RUSTLE! Panic grips the junior lines as men cry out to abandon camp and flee toward Castle Loch before they are completely encircled! 'SHUT UP!'
