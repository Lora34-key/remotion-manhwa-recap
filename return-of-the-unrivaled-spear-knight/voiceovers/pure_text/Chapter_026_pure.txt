Swallowing his terror, Provallum forces a greasy smile, swearing on his life that his underground smugglers will deliver the specimen without fail. Veron strolls out with a parting warning: fail again, and Provallum won't live long enough to enjoy a single silver coin in this empire. Provallum grits his teeth: they have to make it happen, because if they fail, Veron will tear down his entire syndicate brick by bloody brick!
