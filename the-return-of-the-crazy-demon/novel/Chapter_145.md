“No, sir.”

“So Gun-pyeong isn’t in charge because he’s free. You punks take up someone else’s time and don’t even know how to be grateful. So Gun-pyeong.”

“Train them harder. Kick the wacky ones to Ilyang.”

“If any member of the Low-Down causes trouble in Ilyang, the sacred place of the Low-Down Sect, I will be sentenced to immediate death. Take note.”

“Hold up a leg. Whoever puts down his leg will be beaten like yesterday. I’ll give you another chance. Those who want to work can get up and leave the courtyard. “It is an incredible sword formation. And Leader Im is a great commander.”

“What about Black Rabbit Union?”

“Nanming Society is the strongest. The rest is meaningless. Similar to Black Hurricane Castle.”

“Thieving bastards, stand up.”
