I respond with Wooden Fowl Palm Force, then with my back I step onto the furniture in the guest house, which breaks down.

The beggar who got close had spun in the air and retreated.

‘The movements were light.’

In the meantime, the Sword Wind which flew in with the intention of securing a wound on me, began to break the inside of the guest house.

‘Ah, because it is someone else’s guest house, look at them ruining it.’

I get pushed all the way to the kitchen and pull out my dagger with my right hand and use my left hand to grab a random kitchen knife.

I put the wooden sword aside like a trump card for a while. First of all, I put flame qi into the dagger and kitchen knife.

As soon as I saw everything in front of me splitting into a straight line with a low sound, I soar into the air and break down the roof of the guest house.

“…”

Trying to locate the middle-aged man, I say,

“…. But are you all here?”

“What do you mean by that?”

“I was waiting for you to kill all of you. It is a question of whether there are more people who want to die coming here.”

To which the guy smiles.

“This much is enough, I think even after killing the Sect Leader we will have enough strength left.”

I answered walking on the roof that seemed like it would collapse.

“No. That is plain delusion. Those who surrender now will be taken to the Low-Down Sect now. Think well if you want to live. Warrior Yeop. Do you have any thoughts now?”

“Unfortunate. Then you will have to work too hard.”

“Isn’t bow an unfair weapon in Kangho? Insane people.”

The guy is surprised to see me extend my left hand out which holds the kitchen knife and twists his body to the back…

Puak!

“It is meant to hurt.”

“Shut up.”

I smile, stepping back slowly.

“… will you follow if I run?”

“If you are confident, try running.”

I heard a sound from behind which was an arrow to the back of my head.

Swish!

“… ruined.”

Look at the guest house I came into…

And my pursuers come after me.

“You worked pretty hard. I think you will die in the next attack. Poor bastard.”

“Even if I am a mad man, I don’t kill women. Get on your knees fast, I will give a couple slaps and then forgive you. Fools. One fifth of the power you have is lost. As this archer said, I haven’t even done my best yet. Why so?”

Self-Reliance.

When the air begins to turn cold, the chair and table ice over…

After hugging the betrayed lover who was freezing, and watching them under the moonlight…

I suddenly think of such a thing, and my anger rises.

Come to think of it, I was never personally betrayed by anyone.

It was especially because there was no place for loved ones in my life.

“This bastard, look at those eyes!”
