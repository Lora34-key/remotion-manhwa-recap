Waking up in a battered wooden bedroom, our boy Yi Zaha sits up on his straw mattress, blinking in total disbelief as the early morning light filters through the cracked paper windows. This isn’t a fever dream or an illusion before death; he really has returned to his tiny private room at the local inn, stuck inside his twenty-year-old body. Looking down at his bruised arms, he notices the cuts and scrapes littering his skin, and the painful memories of his previous youth instantly flood back into his mind. In his past life, the local street thugs beat him half to death and completely trashed the ground floor of the inn after spreading a fake rumor that Zaha tried to make a move on the town's most popular courtesan, Chae Hyang. It was a completely fabricated setup designed to shake down the innkeeper for protection money, and back then, young Zaha was too weak and terrified to do anything except swallow the humiliation, sweep up the shattered porcelain, and nurse his cracked ribs in silence. But sitting cross-legged on the floor today, Zaha closes his eyes to inspect his internal meridians, expecting an empty, fragile dantian. Instead, his eyes snap open in shock as a warm, heavy river of celestial qi pulses through his veins. The divine energy from the Heavenly Pearl followed his soul through space and time, gifting him a massive cheat code right at level one. Back in his old life, he had to starve, bleed, and meditate in freezing snow for decades just to condense a single droplet of internal force, but now his young vessels are overflowing with refined primordial energy. Without wasting a single second, Zaha begins circulating the Art of the Journey Through the Steel Lakes, his signature cultivation technique from his past life as the Crazy Demon. In a matter of minutes, the dense silver energy clears away his internal bruising, repairs his strained muscles, and reactivates his apex combat instincts. Knowing that having the mind of an unhinged martial grandmaster inside a youthful, qi-filled vessel is practically illegal, Zaha lets out a menacing smirk, steps out of bed, and walks down into the lantern-lit streets of Ilyang Prefecture to handle some unfinished business. The night air is cool and peaceful, but that quiet is immediately ruined when a local thug in silk blue robes spots him walking down the cobblestone road. This arrogant bully was one of the exact goons who jumped Zaha a few days ago, and he immediately starts laughing, loudly mocking our boy for daring to show his face after getting beaten into a pulp. Walking right up into Zaha’s face with peak NPC energy, the thug asks if our boy went completely deaf from the beatdown. Zaha stares blankly at the loudmouth, casually asks for his name, and the split second the thug pauses in confusion, Zaha delivers an explosive backhand slap that spins the man like a top before dropping him face-first into the dirt. The thug’s partner charges forward with wild swings, but Zaha effortlessly ducks beneath the telegraphed punch, clamps his hand over the bully's face like a basketball, and slams his head directly into the stone ground. Standing over the two groaning goons, Zaha shakes his head in disappointment, remarking that the customer service in this neighborhood has truly hit rock bottom. Dusting off his sleeves, our boy turns his attention toward the glowing red lanterns of the Plum Blossom Brothel down the street, kicks the double doors wide open, and announces to the entire gambling parlor that a thirsty customer has officially arrived.
