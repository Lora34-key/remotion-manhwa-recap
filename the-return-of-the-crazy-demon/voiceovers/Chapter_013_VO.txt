Before the blood from the Sword Demon Twins can even settle into the rain gutters, the remaining foot soldiers of the Black Reconnaissance emerge from the dark alleyways, drawing their daggers and surrounding Cha Sung-tae in a desperate attempt to salvage their failed ambush. Completely dropping his tough gangster persona, the brothel manager frantically cowers behind a rain barrel, shrieking like a helpless maiden in distress for our boy to hurry up and save his life. Watching his newly appointed chief financial officer completely abandon all personal dignity, Zaha looks over with deadpan exhaustion, dryly asking if a grown man begging for his life has any shame left in his soul. But instead of letting his subordinate get butchered in the mud, our boy calmly strolls straight into the center of the armed mob, his bare hands resting loosely at his sides. Believing that a scrawny tavern worker is helpless without a forged sword in his grip, the mob enforcers charge forward together, screaming that sheer numbers will overwhelm any lone martial artist. What follows is a brutal masterclass in pure internal palm mechanics: rather than relying on brute physical strength, Zaha channels his refined silver qi directly into his fingertips, using precise kinetic shockwaves that bypass exterior muscle to detonate against internal bone. Without taking a single step backward, our boy unleashes a rapid flurry of concussive palm strikes, cracking against jaws, collarbones, and sternums with terrifying precision. Every strike sends a grown man ragdolling across the wet cobblestones, scattering the entire ambush squad like bowling pins until the alley falls dead silent. The following morning breaks with clear blue skies, the warm golden sunlight washing away the grim violence of the night before. Standing amidst the charred ruins of the Zaha Inn, our boy officially begins the grand reconstruction phase of his underworld empire. He meets with the master builder of the Construction Clan, a legendary guild of craftsmen formerly known as the Chok Sect who specialize in erecting heavily fortified, defensible estates. Looking over the property with keen architectural eyes, the master builder promises to transform the burnt wooden shack into a magnificent martial headquarters that will command respect throughout the province. However, the builder timidly requests a small favor, explaining that his laborers need proper on-site barracks and asking if the new clan leader can occasionally provide hot, nutritious meals to keep morale high during construction. This moment highlights the profound difference between a petty mob boss and a true leader. While other tyrants would threaten the workers with beatings to speed up production, Zaha instantly turns to Cha Sung-tae and issues a clear, generous directive: every single evening, staff from the three local brothels must be dispatched to prepare lavish, hot banquets for the construction crew. In our boy’s grand design, an inn is never just a place to sleep; it is the ultimate continental listening post where traveling martial artists gather to drink, trade gossip, and reveal sect secrets. By treating blue-collar laborers with genuine dignity, decent wages, and warm food, our boy doesn't just hire builders; he earns the absolute, unshakeable loyalty of the working-class backbone of Ilyang Prefecture. Sung-tae smiles with genuine admiration, finally understanding that their young leader is playing a grand, benevolent game of continental influence. But just as the foundation stones are being laid, ominous rumors arrive from the southern highways: the Iron Fist Gang and the high-ranking elders of the Black Rabbit Union have caught wind of the prefecture's sudden silence, and their most brutal enforcers are already mobilizing to reclaim their lost territory with overwhelming, merciless violence. And as the dark storm clouds gather on the horizon, our boy prepares to greet them with open arms.
