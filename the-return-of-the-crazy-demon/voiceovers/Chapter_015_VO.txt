With an earth-shattering battle cry that rattles the mountain rafters, the Golden Phoenix Pavilion Master launches his colossal bulk forward, swinging his massive two-handed executioner’s blade in a catastrophic downward cleave designed to split our boy clean down the middle. In the eyes of ordinary gang enforcers, a two-hundred-and-fifty-pound warlord draped in thick bear pelts wielding a hundred pounds of tempered steel is an unstoppable human juggernaut. But from a tactical and martial perspective, relying purely on colossal weight and wide, telegraphed swings is the hallmark of an amateur who has never fought a true grandmaster; once an attack begins, the inertia prevents the wielder from changing course, leaving their internal organs completely exposed to an inside-line counter. Rather than meeting the crushing blow with a foolish head-on block, Zaha executes the foundational evasive forms of the Steel Lakes technique. Gliding sideways by a mere fraction of an inch, our boy lets the whistling steel obliterate the granite flagstones beneath his boots before drawing his forged blade in a blur of blinding silver light. In a terrifying display of surgical martial precision, Zaha ducks inside the giant's guard, severing the tendon behind his knee, disarming his heavy wrist with an upward flick, and driving a clean, lethal thrust straight through the titan's throat before the dust from the shattered stone can even settle. The colossal Pavilion Master crashes motionless into the dirt with a ground-shaking thud, his terrifying reputation snuffed out in less than three seconds. Absolute, petrified silence descends over the courtyard as dozens of elite pavilion guards freeze in sheer horror, their weapons trembling in their hands. Realizing that the scrawny errand boy just decapitated their supreme commander without breaking a single drop of sweat, the enforcers immediately drop their spears, falling prostrate onto the stone floor and desperately begging for mercy. But this is where we see the darkly hilarious, unhinged legal mind of the Crazy Demon at work. When a nervous officer timidly looks up and asks if they will be spared because they obeyed his order to kneel, Zaha looks down with deadpan confusion, casually pointing out that he promised to execute anyone who refused to kneel, but he never actually agreed to let anyone live just because they bowed. Watching the entire squad of hardened killers short-circuit in sheer existential terror proves that our boy’s sharp tongue and twisted contract logic are just as lethal as his sword. Realizing they are dealing with an unpredictable madman who thrives on psychological chaos, a sharp-eyed strategist steps to the front of the kneeling ranks, boldly arguing that if execution is guaranteed regardless of submission, the surviving soldiers will have no choice but to mount a suicidal last stand and fight to the bitter end. Intrigued by the man's courage and diplomatic composure under extreme pressure, Zaha lowers his blade, asking the negotiator to identify himself and state his proposal. The man bows with professional reverence, introducing himself as Sima Bi, the elite Secret Inspector assigned to monitor the pavilion’s intelligence operations. From an empire-building perspective, this is a monumental acquisition: an organization cannot survive on martial violence alone; it requires an elite administrator who knows the secret ledgers, communication ciphers, and shadow networks of the Black Mystic Union. Recognizing that Sima Bi possesses the exact administrative intellect needed to run a continental spy web, Zaha’s eyes gleam with predatory ambition, realizing that instead of burning down the compound, he just acquired the ultimate brain to guide the Low-Down Sect’s next expansion across the entire southern region, turning a bloody execution into the birth of a continental shadow superpower. And with Sima Bi bowing in genuine allegiance, our boy's network expands exponentially.
