Under the cover of night, heavy wooden wagons loaded with confiscated gold, silver ingots, and secret accounting ledgers roll quietly through the gates of Ilyang Prefecture, marking the successful hostile takeover of the Golden Phoenix Pavilion. Walking beside the convoy, Secret Inspector Sima Bi looks at our boy Yi Zaha with a newfound sense of awe, realizing that this young man didn't just slaughter an arrogant warlord; he systematically dismantled an entire criminal enterprise in less than two hours. But while ordinary gangsters would immediately blow the loot on gambling and expensive wine, Zaha issues a set of strict, unprecedented operational guidelines. He firmly warns Sima Bi that any thug who causes trouble or harasses everyday townspeople will be executed immediately, tasking the new administrator with finding legitimate, honest employment for the dozens of former mob enforcers who have never performed an honest day of work in their lives. In our boy’s vision, the gambling dens and entertainment houses will no longer prey on desperate locals; instead, they are being transformed into legitimate commercial hubs and listening posts where information is harvested cleanly while protecting the vulnerable. When Sima Bi timidly asks what official name they should give to this rapidly expanding organization, Zaha looks up into the starry sky and casually announces the birth of the Matchless Ignoble Clan, otherwise known as the Low-Down Sect. Sima Bi blinks in confusion, admitting he has never heard of such a faction in continental history, to which our boy dryly replies that of course he hasn't, because he literally just made the name up on the spot. Later that evening, the core leadership of the new clan gathers around a crackling bonfire behind the construction site: Cha Sung-tae, Sima Bi, the master blacksmith, and the head of the construction guild. Sitting cross-legged in his crimson robes, Zaha drops his usual comedic banter and delivers a profound philosophical manifesto that completely silences the seasoned criminals around the flames. In the grand, bloody history of Murim, noble righteous sects and demonic cults constantly slaughter each other in majestic mountain duels, but the collateral damage is always paid in the blood of ordinary working people: the tavern waiters who get caught in the crossfire, the carriage drivers robbed on the highways, the cooks beaten for cold soup, and the blacksmiths forced to forge weapons without pay. In the eyes of self-proclaimed martial heroes, the working class is completely invisible, disposable insects whose unjust deaths are never investigated or avenged. But looking around the fire with unyielding conviction, Zaha declares that the Matchless Ignoble Clan exists to turn that broken system upside down. As long as he draws breath, our boy promises that the weak will no longer die in silence; if anyone in their territory harms an innocent commoner, the Low-Down Sect will hunt them down and demand absolute retribution, regardless of how prestigious or powerful the culprit might be. Staring at the twenty-year-old errand boy through the firelight, the cynical brothel manager, the calculating inspector, and the hardened craftsmen feel chills running down their spines. For men who spent their entire lives stabbing each other in the dark for copper coins, being given a transcendent, righteous purpose transforms their loyalty into steel. They realize that their young master isn't just an unhinged demon who enjoys violence; he is a revolutionary force of nature building a sanctuary for the forgotten people of Kangho. But as unity solidifies around the campfire, an ominous shadow stirs deep within the southern mountains, where the supreme leader of the Black Mystic Union has just received a report that completely shatters his composure.
