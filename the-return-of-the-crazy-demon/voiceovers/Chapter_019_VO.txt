Inside the lavish second-floor bedchambers of the Plum Blossom Brothel, an unconscious So Gun-pyeong lies sprawled across a silk mattress, his face swollen and bruised from our boy’s concussive palm strike. Standing at the foot of the bed with his arms crossed, Yi Zaha casually gestures toward the slumbering swordsman and tells Cha Sung-tae to wake him up. What follows is a moment of pure comedic catharsis: having spent days living in terror of regional syndicate assassins, the silver-haired brothel manager seizes the opportunity with hysterical enthusiasm. Relishing the intoxicating power trip of slapping an elite assassin who could have diced him into sushi an hour earlier, Sung-tae furiously pummels the unconscious captain across both cheeks like an over-caffeinated baker kneading dough. Screaming at the top of his lungs, Sung-tae barks that this isn't a luxury vacation resort and demands the intruder wake the hell up. Gasping for air as he jolts awake, So Gun-pyeong clutches his throbbing jaw, staring wide-eyed around the room in absolute bewilderment as he realizes he hasn't been buried in a shallow grave. Standing above him with an unblinking, analytical stare, Zaha gets straight to business, cutting through all pleasantries to interrogate the captain regarding the current whereabouts of the Great Rakshasa, the terrifying slaughterer of the Twelve Heavenly Palms. So Gun-pyeong swallows nervously, admitting that even the supreme Union Master rarely knows the Slaughterer's movements, as the grandmaster wanders Kangho as an autonomous force of pure devastation. Seeing that the swordsman has exhausted his immediate strategic value, our boy shifts into executive recruitment mode, casually offering So Gun-pyeong the position of Chief Combat Instructor for the newly formed Matchless Ignoble Clan. The elite nodachi master stares at Zaha like he just lost his mind, completely dumbfounded that a hardened assassin is being asked to train tavern waiters, kitchen scullions, and brothel security guards in military swordplay. But this is where Zaha deploys his signature weapon of aggressive psychological coercion. He coldly explains that So Gun-pyeong has two distinct career options: he can reject the offer, walk out the front door, and attempt to explain to the Black Mystic Union why he lost his squad, surrendered his territory, and handed over his prized nodachi, which will result in his immediate, agonizing execution for treason; or, he can accept the instructor job, enjoy three hot meals a day, receive full legal immunity under the Crazy Demon's protection, and help train the most unhinged shadow syndicate the continent has ever seen. Faced with the undeniable reality that loyalty to the union equals a gruesome death sentence while working for the crazy errand boy offers survival and decent benefits, So Gun-pyeong’s pride evaporates, and he bows his head in reluctant submission. This acquisition completes the trifecta of Zaha’s core organization: Sima Bi commands intelligence, Cha Sung-tae manages finances, and So Gun-pyeong oversees martial training. Looking at his assembled command staff, Zaha realizes that while they are all deeply flawed criminals, their shared fear of his wrath binds them tighter than any sacred oath. But before the ink can dry on the new instructor’s employment contract, chilling news echoes through the prefecture’s outer outposts: the dreaded Twin Ghosts of the Ringed Blade have officially entered the city gates. Armed with terrifying Nine-Ring Sabers that emit hypnotic acoustic vibrations designed to rupture an opponent's internal qi, these notorious executioners have come with direct orders to slaughter every living soul connected to the Zaha Inn, leaving our boy smiling as he unsheathes his steel to show them why true terror has no face, setting up an immediate street battle that will shock the entire province.
