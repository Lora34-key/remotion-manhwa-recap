The tension in the Black Rabbit Union courtyard reaches an absolute boiling point as the supreme leader draws his dark sword from its scabbard, realizing that unless he cuts down this unhinged errand boy in front of his entire syndicate, his authority is permanently dead. Right as the duel is about to ignite, So Gun-pyeong steps forward from the perimeter and solemnly offers his prized Night Blade to Zaha, formally acknowledging him as Leader for the very first time. Zaha takes the legendary nodachi with a smirk, joking that while it is slightly better than a kitchen cleaver, it will do the job just fine. What follows is a breathtaking display of high-level martial mechanics. The Union Leader attacks with a masterclass of refined, deceptive swordplay, switching seamlessly between offense and defense while laying subtle tactical traps to exploit false openings. But Zaha does not fight like a textbook martial artist; his combat patterns are fluid, indefinite, and completely formless. Instead of rushing for an immediate execution, Zaha deliberately matches the leader blow for blow, using his supreme regression insight to memorize every cadence and sword angle of Dae Na-chal's regional style. As the clash passes thirty trades, Zaha gradually unleashes the true depths of the internal qi he cultivated from the Heavenly Pearl, coating the Night Blade in roaring crimson heat. The Union Leader's face pales behind his mask as he realizes with suffocating dread that this teenage errand boy has been toying with him using less than half of his true cultivation. Cornered like a wild animal, the boss desperately unleashes a lethal wave of condensed sword energy aimed directly toward Gun-pyeong's men, hoping to force Zaha to dodge and kill the defectors. But Zaha does not dodge; he steps straight forward and unleashes the Fiery Fragrance, a blazing tidal wave of Fire Chicken Qi that violently obliterates the incoming blade aura, shattering the leader's sword and blasting his body thirty feet backward into the stone fortress wall with bone-crushing force. The Union Leader is left half-buried in the cracked masonry, vomiting dark blood down the front of his wooden mask like a morbid fresco painting. Zaha turns to the petrified garrison and nonchalantly declares that the errand boy won, offering to slaughter anyone who feels like avenging their boss, but not a single enforcer dares to breathe. Walking over to the dying leader, Zaha pulls off the black rabbit mask, revealing a trembling, broken face whispering for mercy. Zaha coldly tells him that surviving means abandoning martial arts to live as a humble tavern errand boy, washing dishes and wiping tables until a random swordsman cuts him down, the true tragic reality of everyday commoners in the Jianghu. Before the man can even process the offer, his final breath escapes. Zaha turns around, takes the rabbit mask dripping with fresh crimson blood, places it firmly onto his own face, and declares to the paralyzed syndicate that from this day forward, he is the new supreme leader of the Black Rabbit Union, whether they like it or not, because that is simply what he decided. With the entire underworld garrison kneeling in terrified submission and the terrifying twelve regional generals preparing to march on Ilyang Prefecture, the real war for the continent has officially begun.
